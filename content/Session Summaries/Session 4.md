The party hides out in Lurtz' house and realizes Salazar had snuck away from the party some time earlier. suddenly a loud banging rings the wooden door and a parchment paper of their newly acquired WANTED status is shoved underneath the door gap. They are alerted of the lower tier shutdown.

A man stops by Lurtz' house asking for Zink, but they lie and tell the man that he isn't here. He doesn't believe them, but unencouraged to press further, leaves his business card and tells them they can reach out if they'd like.

The group decides to go pay a visit to the undertown of Guttertown in disguises to sell some of their recently acquired goods, where they make 300g off of 2 platinum bars, 50g off of a crate of iron ore, and about 30g off of 3 bushels of an unknown weed.

Meanwhile, Zink hits rock bottom mentally. He contemplates suicide and comes to a realization that perhaps the corporate life isn't for him, but still wrestles with his inner conflict. In search of answers, he seeks out the hallucinogen Marbleweed. Walking aimlessly towards the dock warrens, he sees a skate park where he meets an emo gnome who offers him some Marbleweed for 2g. Zink takes it all immediately. On his way back to proper Guttertown, the same man approaches Zink, confusing him for another party member in his disguise, but tells him that if he sees Zink, he was instructed to come offer him a promotion for the good work he did at the factory. He mentions that he can get his name cleared of wanted status and wishes the man to inform Zink of this urgently.

Meanwhile, Elysia confronts her father as he is given a letter by a gaunt, pale, elven man bearing the T&C sigil on his suit jacket cufflinks. After an argument, she goes upstairs, where she is to be kept under watch by the servants in the house. She begins to make for the window as she usually does, but Merrick, a halfling wizard comes in and stops her, recasting his alarm spell at the window to be alerted of any further attempts to escape. She asks to be granted access to the library, and her father comes in yet again granting her supervised access to the library, but that she is grounded for 2 months, upon which she may begin to get her freedoms back.

Hopping back over to the other group, Zink begins tripping hard balls. He sees visions of his life flash before his eyes in Instagram reels playing out on a brick wall beside him. Eventually, he snaps out briefly and is forced to wrestle with his inner conflict of whether he truly wants to die or not. Suddenly, a piercing pain strikes him in the back, as multiple sets of teeth dig their way through his armor. A balled, viscous, pulsing amalgamation of flesh and mouths and eyes lurches at him and sinks sets of protruding teeth into his backside. 

Luckily, the rest of the gang also sees this at the right time, and they enter combat. Bada boom strikes heavy with his greatclub, splashing fleshy viscera along either side of the alleyway. Suddenly, the creature recoalesces into 2 smaller creatures, attempting to slink back into the shadows. Bada Bing is able to wrap his shirt around one and dangles it in the air, but not for long. As it wrestles its way out of Bing's weak grip, it escapes and assails Lurtz one more time before being smitten down by the wrath of Bada Boom's greatclub, searing it into a pile of blood and ash, leaving behind only a pulsating blue tinted stone with gold encrusted accents. The other creature climbs the nearby roof of a building and begins squirming away as quickly as it can, leaping rooftop to rooftop across the shanty district of Guttertown. 
Bada Boom acts quickly, climbing the roof and offering a rope to his companions to quickly yank them in hot pursuit. Bada Bing and Zink hold strong, but Lurtz loses grip just as Boom yanks the rope and goes flying through a glass window of the building they are atop.

Inside, Lurtz awakens a dwarf who reaches for a knife at the home invader and attempts to stab the disoriented goblin. Lurtz deftly disengages the dwarf, and taunts him before joining his friends -- but not before the dwarf vows to hunt Lurtz down and kill him himself.

Before long, Bada Bing and boom follow after the fleshy creature and, in an attempt to smash it, accidentally put 2 large cracks/holes in the thatch roof of a house, where he awakens 3 (now screaming) children from within. Lurtz following behind, misses the jump to the rooftop and accidentally falls into one of the holes made by Bada Boom's greatclub, where he is surrounded by 3 young kobolds, as the mother and father kobolds crash through the door. The mother protects the children while the father, holding a pitchfork, attacks the home invader, getting 1 good stab before Lurtz can make it to the door, maneuvering around the man. Lurtz climbs back up the building, but possibly concussed from his early falls, missteps into the other hole Bada Boom placed and falls back into the childrens' room. This time, the children arm themselves and Lurtz takes a few good stabs from the father and some of the children before making a mad dash for the exit and once again climbing up to meet up with his friends. 

Bada Bing is able to make the creature take pause for a moment, as he pretends to be the creatures' mother. At this moment, he instructs Bada Boom to seize this opportunity and splatters the creature, soaking viscera into the thatch roof as they loot the second pulsating blue orb.
