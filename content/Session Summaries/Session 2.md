Lurtz, Elysia, Salazar, and Zink found a hole in the fence surrounding the perimeter of the factory. They send Bing and Boom to cause a diversion so they could break in. They both ran around the sides of the facility and began amassing a crowd to riot against the guards, a public outcry that they are keeping them out. The guards try to detain the two, but decide they aren't doing much wrong yet and just sit near them instead, afraid of the big bugbear.

Lurtz stealthily opens one of the loading bay doors and begins to lift when it suddenly takes control of the small goblin and slams upward, taking Lurtz with it and causing a loud racket, alerting all of the nearby guards as well as a mechanic and guard in the receiving section of the building. The guards begin to fight, and Bing and Boom are able to keep them at bay for the most part while Lurtz convinces the mechanic to fix the lift with the rope he gave him and lower them down. The captain of the guard comes down after hearing the commotion, but Boom gets the better of him, searing his entire body with his greatclub imbued with divine smite. 

During the carnage, a reporter approaches bada bing to get the scoop on what is going down here in the Brassreach Yards in front of the abandoned [[Olostina Etherworks]]' Factory 17, providing the names of Zink Mangrove, and others accompanying the infiltration.

Bing and Boom request a wheelbarrow from a nearyby hardware store and use it to collect all they can and begin to haul it back to Lurtz's house in Guttertown.

Meanwhile, the rest of the gang decide to descend into the basement floor to put in the lockdown code where they learn there have been hidden experiments performed on various humanoids etc. They learned of at least 5 different types of test subjects that are nowhere to be seen. In the control room is a slow-moving cyclone of debris being upheld by the telekinetic creature in front of the control panel, channeling some kind of essence from the large silos along the walls. 
 Carefully, Lurtz ascends the metal catwalk up to where the control panel is just in front of the oily, pale white humanoid currently channeling some spell. As he stealthily reaches past the body of this creature to input the code silently, he goes to pull the lever. At that moment, the humanoid's eyes open and dart to the goblin as he speaks: "Witness me, little goblin! You will not thwart my efforts!"
