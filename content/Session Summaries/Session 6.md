The party was escorted to jail cells for holding while being processed for their crimes in the Clockspire borough. In the cells, they met a tattooed Earth Genasi who expressed to Zink that T&C was a bad company, and that he was arrested and being sent to the Iron Mines for attempting to hold on to his memories of his own employment with T&C. He showed them some burn marks and tried to warn Zink that the company is just using them and they may suffer a similar fate soon enough. They also met an Elf druid named Burgedorf III who, as an empath and activist, decided she ought to help these people.

Bada Bing, Elysia, and Goblino are released from jail after having no charges stick. A T&C representative [[Silas Vane]], arrives to inform Bada Bing that the judge has been paid off, but to be able to keep appearances of plausible deniability, they will likely need someone of innate authority to vouch for the bugbear. They devise a plan to find a member of the Grinning Gang (or whatever) at a nearby inn, called the [[Mermaid's Keep]] in the Clockspire to figure out where Elysia lives to be able to get her to vouch. A hooded goliath ([[Hermes Handlefoot]]) approaches and expresses that he in a member of [[The Greywater Syndicate]], that should they need assistance in the future, they could seek out the group and their cause is a righteous one. He helps what he can by offering to draw a map to where he knows the Olostina estate to be located.

They arrive at the estate, and Borgedarf III transforms into a bat to sneak in the window and rescue Elysia from being grounded in her room. She wild shapes into an octopus and pulls Elysia out as suddenly Merrick, the Olostina house-wizard, attempts to rescue her from her captors. Failing miserably, he stands dejected, unable to chase at the estate border as the party runs off into the misty night. They rendezvous back to [[Mermaid's Keep]] and stay a night, forcing [[Goblino]] to sleep outside, out of fear of catching his leprosy. The next morning, Goblino is nowhere to be seen, with only a newspaper bedding behind the inn and a severed finger tagged with the Three Daggers sigil of [[The Greywater Syndicate]] left behind. 

With more pressing matters, the group arrives back at the jailhouse in time to learn that the court hearing has been expedited by the guard that [[Bada Boom]] hit as an attempt to get him booked in time for the prison wagon arrival the next day. 

Zink, Lurtz, and Bada Boom are all tried within the same hearing, a very unconventional way, and represented by Bada Bing. With Bing's exceptional legalese, and a little edge from the judge, he is able to squelch the plaintiff and get all charges from Boom dropped, and gets Zink and Lurtz down to a measly 100g fine. 

Zink returns to work the next day, and although he got reprimanded and a 25% wage garnishment to pay off the representative they had to send and for damage control costs, he is given a task.

Rumors are that some folks in The Brassreach Yards and the East Slums outside the city have been encountering strange, scary monstrosities of flesh, tooth, and eyes; coagulations of flesh from the evacuation at the factory they destroyed. They are to use the memory wiping tool IT provided them and clean up after the mess with the utmost discretion and urgency; even the slightest sniff of trouble getting found out by the Beaurocrats could get T&C in some deep shit. They are to investigate the rumors, and pursue any leads that seem suspicious, all the while keeping the cleanup on the down-low and memory wiping anyone who's sees something they ought-not to.
