The most prominent of [[The Dregahn Holdfasts]], House Caerwyn is notable for its willingness to engage with the outside world—a rarity among the fiercely independent mountain tribes. Based in the mist-wreathed fortress of Caerwyn’s Hollow, the house has thrived by brokering rare minerals, spirit-forged weapons, and alchemical reagents. Their ancestral pacts with the land’s guardian spirits make them both respected and feared.

Lady [[Mairead Caerwyn]] currently heads the house, a stoic and cunning matriarch rumored to speak with the dead. Her two sons, [[Aedin Caerwyn|Aedin]] and [[Corwin Caerwyn|Corwin]], manage external trade and spiritual rites respectively. Though their influence is limited by geography and tribal rivalry, House Caerwyn holds unmatched sway among the Holdfasts.
