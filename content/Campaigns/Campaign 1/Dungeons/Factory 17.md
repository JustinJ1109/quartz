
## 🛑 **The Factory 17 Incident**

About **four days ago**, Factory 17 was operating as usual — extracting volatile magical essences from rare ingredients smuggled out of forbidden lands and processed using _living etheric vessels_ (aka prisoners, vagrants, and debtors). This operation, part of T&C’s **Project Harrowglass**, was designed to refine a new type of arcane energy known as **“Mirrorflux”** — unstable, hyper-potent magic capable of being weaponized or powering experimental constructs.

**The problem:**  
R&D scientists, under pressure from upper management to accelerate production, cut safety protocols during a live siphoning trial. One of the “subjects” — a malformed humanoid saturated with magical exposure — reacted violently, triggering a **cascading Mirrorflux feedback surge**. This sent a blast of wild, reality-warping energy through half the plant.

**Immediate consequences:**

- Dozens of workers and test subjects were **instantly vaporized** or turned into eldritch horrors.
    
- Arcane containment wards failed.
    
- Several constructs malfunctioned, attacking indiscriminately.
    
- Municipal Watch was called in, but T&C quickly cordoned off the site.
    
- The factory was evacuated, and a hasty story about a _“chemical fire”_ was pushed to the press.
    

---

## ⚠️ **Why T&C Needs the Evidence Gone**

The Mirrorflux incident left behind:

- **R&D records** implicating T&C in unethical live siphoning experiments.
    
- **Surviving mutated horrors** in containment.
    
- The experimental **Mirrorflux core** — dangerously unstable, if found could expose everything.
    
- Test logs naming high-ranking officials who authorized Project Harrowglass.
    

If the Municipal Watch — or worse, the Assembly — discover the scope of these experiments and the factory’s role in the illegal energy trade, it could:

- Spark public outrage.
    
- Collapse stock value.
    
- Lead to Assembly investigations implicating T&C execs.
    
- Empower rival industrial barons to seize T&C’s assets.
    

So… the order came down:  
**“Initiate Clearing House.”**  
Wipe the records. Destroy the assets. Leave no trace. The factory is a liability, and anyone who knows what happened inside must be… dealt with.




## Layout
### 📍 **GROUND FLOOR**

**1️⃣ Front Office & Break Room**

- Dusty desks, old paperwork, toppled chairs, half-eaten food.
    
- A flickering magelamp.
    
- **Clue:** A manager's logbook mentions "shipment delays" and "R&D irregularities."
    
- **Encounter:** Possibly a lone nervous **Municipal Watch guard** on patrol (DC 14 Stealth to avoid).
    

---

**2️⃣ Main Manufacturing Floor**

- Conveyor belts, vats of strange minerals and ichor.
    
- Abandoned carts and upended containers.
    
- **Hazard:** Thin magical residue — minor arcane surges (DC 12 Con save or 1d4 force damage as residual magic flares).
    

---

**3️⃣ Loading Dock & Maintenance Hatch**

- Large dock doors chained shut from the outside.
    
- **Maintenance Hatch #43** partially open, leading down to **Level B1**.
    
- **Clue:** A bloodied T&C badge on the ground, marked _R&D Access Only_.
    

---

**4️⃣ Security Office**

- Two defunct magitek surveillance orbs.
    
- A shattered sending stone.
    
- **Treasure:** Map of Factory 17 (revealing basement access points and "Substation Theta" noted on B2).
    
- **Encounter:** A trapped arcane glyph explodes for 2d6 fire damage if opened carelessly (DC 14 Investigation to notice).
    

---

**5️⃣ R&D Division Wing (Behind Steel Door)**

- Heavy steel door requires **R&D badge** or DC 16 lockpick check.
    
- Inside: Tables lined with half-disassembled creatures, arcane extraction devices, jars of unsettling things.
    
- **Clue:** Lab notes detailing “ether siphoning from living subjects” and a mention of Project Harrowglass.
    
- **Encounter:** One unstable containment pod — a malformed creature (Homebrew: _Spell-Twisted Aberrant_, CR 1) breaks free if disturbed.
    

---

### 📍 **BASEMENT LEVEL B1**

**6️⃣ Freight Lift Control Room**

- Rusted lift car stuck halfway down.
    
- Controls jury-rigged — requires successful DC 13 Intelligence (Tinker’s Tools) to bypass.
    
- **Hazard:** Malfunctioning lift may drop violently (Dex save DC 13 to avoid 2d6 bludgeoning).
    

---

**7️⃣ Storage Bay B1-A**

- Crates labeled "Arcane Hazard — Handle With Care".
    
- **Treasure:** Alchemical reagents worth 50 gp.
    
- **Clue:** Scribbled note: _“Move remaining assets to Theta before sweep.”_
    
- **Encounter:** Swarm of animated scrap constructs (CR 1).
    

---

**8️⃣ Substation Access Tunnels**

- Dark, claustrophobic crawlspaces.
    
- **Hazard:** Leaking pipes — scalding steam jets (Dex save DC 13 or 1d6 fire).
    
- **Encounter:** A crazed former worker hiding here (Paranoid, babbling about “the hungry things in the substation”).
    

---

### 📍 **BASEMENT LEVEL B2**

**9️⃣ Control Substation Theta**

- Central control room: sparking consoles, glowing arcane cores.
    
- **Objective:** Input override sequence **R-7X-12-LOCKDOWN** into main console.
    
- Primary lever labeled **“RELEASE SEQUENCE”**.
    
- **Encounter:** A final guardian construct (Magitek Warden, CR 2) activates on override attempt.
    [Steel Defender](https://5e.tools/bestiary/stone-defender-mtf.html)

---

**🔟 Containment Vault (Optional Room)**

- Sealed chamber next to Substation Theta.
    
- Inside: Suspended containment pods holding horrific magical constructs, failed experiments, and a half-dissolved corpse.
    
- **Treasure:** A prototype arcane device (your call — maybe a single-use wand or unstable potion).
    
- **Clue:** A logbook hinting at a visiting dignitary or exec scheduled to tour the site next week.

## 🎲 Dungeon Notes:

- The players should have options for stealth, social deception, minor combats, and environmental hazards.
    
- The deeper they go, the more the horrors of T&C’s practices become apparent.
    
- Substation Theta is the mission critical point — pulling the lever triggers a factory-wide arcane purge protocol, setting up a dangerous timed escape sequence if you want.
