## Entrance
#### Cranium Rat Swarm
in the entrance, a swarm of rats is currently feeding on a fleshy clump of glowing meat, they speak telepathically, and the party, as they approach, can hear the rats talk cordially among each other.
The rats have been luring some gullible citizens down here for the flesh creature as they worship it, claiming it is a piece of God and that it offers them the gift of knowledge. If the party threatens to kill the flesh creature, the rats will attempt to attack and call for help.
