Heiress to a baron of T&C
Born an heir to inherit her father's business: [[Olostina Etherworks]]. Her parents own multiple factories responsible for R&D of [[Thresher & Coil Industry]]'s magic item production.

Intro Hook: Her parents constantly warned her to stay far from the lower tiers of [[Greyharbor]], insisting that the misfortunes of the "lesser folk" were contagious, and it wasn’t her place to meddle in the lives of those "too lazy to lift themselves up." What they never realized was that, for years, she’d been sneaking down to the **Lowdocks** taverns, chasing cheap drink and rough company alongside a close friend — part rebellion, part curiosity, and part craving for a life that wasn’t gilded and suffocating.
