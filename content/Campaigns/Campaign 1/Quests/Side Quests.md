**1. The Collector’s Request**  
_Hook:_ The infamous underbelly dentist known as _The Collector_ sends word through a street kid that he’s paying triple for any “premium hardware” — but this time, it’s not just fillings. Rumor has it a group of dead men were pulled from the East Slums canal last week, all missing their teeth. The Collector wants someone to track down who’s encroaching on his trade, and “return the inventory.”  
_Leads to:_ A rival back-alley mortician named _Graves Malone_ running a corpse-smuggling ring for experimental purposes… funded by a shadowy third party with T&C ties.

**2. Dead Man’s Message**  
_Hook:_ The pirate player hears a rumor in the Lowdocks that _Vargan Voss_, an old shipmate presumed dead after a T&C security ambush, was seen alive — raving about something he stole from the wrong cargo manifest. He left a message scratched into the underside of a dock piling.  
_Leads to:_ Vargan’s half-decayed corpse hidden in a warehouse icebox, clutching a key to a smuggler’s vault containing manifest ledgers T&C _really_ doesn’t want public.

**3. Stitched and Screaming**  
_Hook:_ Homeless folk from Guttertown have been disappearing in droves. A pair of gutter orphans (_Ratlick Tommy_ and _Ginger Jo_) claim their friend was grabbed by men in black aprons and never came back. One night, the PCs hear inhuman shrieks echoing from a sealed-off sewer tunnel near Brassreach.  
_Leads to:_ A hidden vivisection chamber used by T&C’s Olostina Etherworks subsidiary for experimental augmentation trials on vagrants.

**4. The Guttertown Races**
_Hook:_ The annual Rustbucket Races — an illegal scrap-cart derby through Greyharbor’s underworks — is coming up. There’s a huge prize purse and rumor that one of the racers is smuggling an info-chip on T&C’s next major operation.
Catch: The Guttertown gangs are violently territorial over the course, and sabotage is expected.
