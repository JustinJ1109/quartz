[[Session Summaries/Session 3|Session 3 Summary]]

### Hot on The Run
The party hides out in Lurtz's house, deciding their next moves. As they do, they hear a loud banging on the door and a piece of paper slides under the doorway. 

"By Order of Magistrate Orvus Grumblatch, all citizens of Brassreach Yards, Guttertown and the Outer Slums will be subjected to otherwise unlawful search and seizure to assist in the search of 5 highly wanted individuals. All access to and from the upper tiers or outside the city will be restricted until further notice. Anyone with knowledge that would assist in the search of these individuals will be rewarded."

They soon see a mix of city guards and district guards patrolling the streets and entering into peoples' homes (there is a distinct difference in the budget, as the city guard appear to be magically trained and are armored in a kind of enchanted plate). A few offer a courtesy knock, while other groups simply burst the door in and enter.

#### Captured
If the party gets captured, they will be brought to jail to await an expedited trail.  
Their lawyer comes in: Regis Flintrock -- An unusually tall green kobold (is actually 2 kobolds in a trench coat) in a twill trench coat, a clearly fake mustache and black haired wig, with a lip full of chew. The first thing the party notices is an odd gait about him, every step seems to throw him off balance as he throws a briefcase on the table and spits the chew out. "Greetings from the Collector, fellas. Look, I'll keep it real for yous guys. It's not looking good, but I'll do what I can for ya's."

The trail happens after a day or so. Regis makes the best case he can, but with the zone of truth and many eye witnesses called to the stand, it's a very difficult battle and the jury will almost certainly rule guilty and they will be ruled put to death.

If this happens, Zink discovers that a liaison from T&C's [[Thresher & Coil Industry#CL&CED|CL&CED]] team has gone to the jail to request a meeting. If Zink agrees to the [[Zink NDA|NDA]], and terms for further service, his bail will be paid. He can persuade the man to extend the offer to his partners if he wishes but they must sign the NDA and be willing to work for T&C for 3 years.

#### The Collector
If the party goes to [[The Collector]], He informs the players that the lockdown has put a wrench in some urgent shipments he had planned to go out tonight. The party notices that while the collector typically outwardly portrays a happy, calm demeanor, he seems annoyed and even slightly angry.

#### Intercepted by T&C
A member of T&C's [[Thresher & Coil Industry#CL&CED|CL&CED]] team intercepts Zink alone, if possible. They will inform him that the [[Factory 17]] fiasco has been made much harder to clean up than anticipated, but that because of their actions, the Greyharbor Workers' Rights Union case on unethical research and testing no longer holds any water, and the lawyers have begun a countersuit for defamation. So despite the loudness of it all, Zink is praised and they would like to offer him a position as "Team Leader" of the [[Thresher & Coil Industry#SRCD|SRCD]] team. Of course, it comes with stricter confidentiality policies and there's the matter of the [[Thresher & Coil Industry#PIMB|PIMB]] team to clean up the public image / erase their wanted status. 

If Zink agrees to accept the job position, he will be laterally promoted from contractor of [[Olostina Etherworks]] to full-time [[Thresher & Coil Industry]] salaried employee with a 3rd floor corner office on the corner of the [[Greyharbor#🕰️ The Clockspire — Government Core & Bureaucracy Labyrinth|Clockspire]] overlooking the river in the T&C Headquarter building, and a generous new salary of 1,000gp/year (was previously making 200gp/yr).

He is given a visitor badge and instructed to show up at his new place of employment the following day at 8am.

#### Elysia Escorted Home
When Elysia arrives at her family's estate, she is escorted inside the gates, where she sees an unfamiliar horse-drawn carriage in the driveway. 2 men which she recognizes to be her father's security stand on either side of the doorway. inside a few more members of the security team keep watch in the house. It seems her father has stepped up security since last she saw; her father isnt usually a paranoid person and only 1 guard is typically present in the house. Her father, [[Arthur Olostina]], sets down a note he just received from a slim, pale man with slicked back auburn hair bearing the T&C logo on his suit cufflinks who gives a slight bow of gratitude and walks out. 

He sternly scolds his daughter, "My daughter! What were you thinking? Tarnishing the family name by slinking around with those vermin -- those... _gutterfilth_. It is no place for one esteemed such as yourself. I give you strict orders to keep to the Gildsprawl -- you go to classes and come home to study. What if you were hurt? All the more reason to keep a stricter watch on you. From now on, you are not to leave this house. I will arrange for your classes to be delivered privately here in the safety of our home"

##### The Note
The slim, pale man Marrek Veylin leaves Arthur Olostina with a wax-sealed message.
It reads:

“Arthur,
Your shipments from the northern line have been delayed longer than projected. This reflects poorly on your reliability as a supplier. The Board expects delivery before quarter’s end, or an alternative explanation. In the meantime, we suggest you make the security of your household a priority. The streets below Gildsprawl teem with unrest, and certain… anomalies may seek the warmth of higher fires. Consider this a courtesy.
— M.V.”

#### Zink's First Day
Upon arrival, he will be granted his badge, sit through orientation, and then told where his office is located. He has his own receptionist, Arnold, a goliath. Arnold informs him of a meeting in 3 minutes on the top floor.

Zink walks into a boardroom where the same pale man (Marrek Veylin, CL&CED liaison) is presenting. The board is reviewing damage control from Factory 17:

The Union lawsuit is neutralized thanks to the “arson narrative.”

Public unrest in Brassreach/Guttertown has spiked → [[Thresher & Coil Industry#PIMB|PIMB]] is rolling out propaganda.

Problem: One experiment, The Mawspawn, escaped containment and has been spotted crossing over the bridge to the Clockspire district.

Until the PIMB teams can silence down the rumors of the Factory completely, it wouldn't be all that hard for people to draw their own conclusions where such a creature came from, which would certainly cut into our bottom line.

If upper-class families panic, it undercuts T&C’s entire “we bring progress and stability” narrative.

So the board looks at Zink:

as new team leader of the [[Thresher & Coil Industry#SRCD|SRCD]], are to reassemble what remains of your task force, and to eliminate signs of these creatures throughout the city. The gildsprawl is by far the most important to cleanse first. There has been a sighting near the Aureal Vineyard in the Gildsprawl, that should be their first target after obtaining the biomass filtrator.


Once you have gathered your group, head down to the CL&CED building on the corner of Royal&First ave in the guildsprawl to collect a carepackage that will be vital to the success of this mission. It includes the Biomass Filtrator you will use, as well as a sending stone which will be used to coordinate any further sightings that must be handled.

“Mr. Mangrove, your… unconventional methods at Factory 17 have impressed us. You and your team will form the spearpoint of our Special Risk Containment Department. Your first assignment is simple: discreetly track and eliminate the escaped specimen. Should you fail, many could die. And worse, our Q3 expected will fall short and shareholders will be left questioning. We will divert attention as best as possible while you and your group handle the situation _with discretion_ this time... We cannot afford another incident like the Brassreach Brawl, as the low-folk are calling it. This is a matter of utmost discreetness. In the case that the creature kills anyone or can be traced back to T&C, we will need you to id the victim and record their names in this ledger. We will send a doppelganger from the Department of Continuity & Replacement to fill in.”

### Tracking the Monster
The creatures are attracted to magical essence. Specifically, drunken mages.
It will attempt to feed in the shadows.

Gildsprawl - Aureal Vineyard

Upon arrival, the party notices there is some kind of event happening, and a large group of drunken mages are currently wine tasting midday. 
A wine server comes from a cellar below and begins to divvy out glasses to the groups. Party notices one of the mages seems to imbue a coin and hand it to the man who accepts it graciously and heads back to the cellar.

In the cellar, the party will find the wine servant torn apart, ripped from nipple to naval, and his pants torn apart. INT check to connect to 2.

Clockspire - Velvet Quill Inn.
A dorky wizard claims he heard a group of otherworldly voices behind him while he was taking a shortcut through the alley by Fifth and Melvin, but booked it.

Guttertown - Old Dock Warrens - located on the edge of Undertown near the river. This area was long abandoned but Badabing heard rumors of this area being a common place for dark magic rituals, seedy deals by aristocrats to criminals, as well as a more recently refurbished skate park that a lot of the local kids hang out around. In fact, it's where Bing witnessed his first death.
