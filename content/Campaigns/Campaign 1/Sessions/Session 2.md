### Factory Infiltration
Notes that the party learned during the casing of the factory:
- The facility is surrounded by a chainlink fence with barbed wire on the top roughly 15 feet from the building.
- Roughly 12 guards patrolling in pairs of 2s in the daytime and likely more inside, give the number of patrol caravans and horses stabled off near the front of the building.
- 2 Guards stand watch at the front entrace on the **South** side of the factory: a locked gate that they pull open and closed that leads to a 3 step stair to the front office of the building.
- Along the **West** side of the building is a street that leads to the riverwalk to the north about 60 feet. A number of mom-and-pop shops, hardware stores, liquor shops, smoke shops, etc line the other side of the street here. A small goblin-sized door is built into the wall nearest the back side of the building. (maintenance stairwell entrance to the basement)
- About 60 feet away from the river sits the **North** side of the building (the back of the building) and is the most wide open area. Where a block of houses and shops would be is instead an open dirt parcel of land that is used to bring in supplies to the factory, and a popular smoking area for factory workers, as [[Lurtz]] would know. A handful of supply crates sit there stacked. Some empty, others looked like they were abandoned during the quarantine half-unloaded. This area is enclosed in the fence as well.
- Along the East side of the building is an alley sandwiched between the factory and the backside of some other buildings. Notably, it appears that part of the fence had been torn open here and then patched back together with some kind of white rope. There are no doors on this side of the factory at floor level, but there are a 4 windows on a smaller upper floor directly ahead of the patch in the fence, one of which was shattered.

### Main Floor
#### Offices
Reception area on the south side of the building, punch-clock, desks, etc.
A back room behind the clerks desk that leads to a stairwell upstairs to the [[#Manager's Room]] and a main threshold to the [[#Main shop|shop floor]].

#### Manager's Room
Upstairs, 4 windows facing the east alley. 1 window is shattered. Contains a safe (DC 14 thieves tools).

#### Main shop
Large open space with marble flooring with conveyer belts, clear tubes with stagnant fluid sitting at the bottom of various luminescent colors.
A number of workstations and desks surround the outer walls, and a half-wall seperates the main shop from the [[#Receiving Dock]] in the back.
Crates are organized neatly at various workstations, though it is clear that some workers were more or less tidy than others.

A number of chutes along the walls open up where materials are sent upon completion as Lurtz is familiar.

`3 guards`

#### Receiving Dock
3 large garage metal doors with handles open to a conveyer system. Stacks of all types of crates, some wooden, others metal or stone. Back here is much more scattered and seems to be more organized in its own disorganization. On the east side of the building is an open gate protecting an elevator lift to the basement. Only the winch itself and the mechanical apperatus that guides the lift is visible, the carriage itself is already descended. The teeth of two of the gears have been grinded down, no longer functional. It's as if the elevator had rapidly descended either on purpose, or accident. The cable seems frayed, but seems to be holding taught. A toolbox is laid down beside, it appears the guards were working on repairing the lift to investigate further but were unable to complete.

`2 guards and a goblin mechanic.`

A number of stacked crates marked with Sigils that notate "Arcane Hazard", company logos from the nearby dwarven mining companies.

DC 12 Investigation to notice that the marble here has been chipped and cracked, almost the shape of talons or claws, each about as long as their heads.

#### Basement
Narrow, flickering hallways lit by arcane invention. The inside is damp like a cave, now that all the ventilation has been non-functional for a few days. A doorway opens up into a holding chamber. 6 reinforced steel cells align the hallway walls. Blood smears on the walls scribble in a foreign language (Celestial) "The Witness watches, even now." A side office filled with filing cabinets behind a desk. see [[Dossier 1]], [[Dossier 2]], [[Dossier 3]]
