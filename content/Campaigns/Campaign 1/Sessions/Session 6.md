### Session Opening
Players are being transported to jail and held for trial.

Bada Bing - Accomplice in crime. Will be questioned but probably released.
Bada Boom - Assault on city watch guard. Will almost certainly be shipped off to prison on the next prison wagon, unless they can get him off on that he is medically stupid, in which case one of his party members will need to register as his legal guardian to be held responsible by his actions
Lurtz - Breaking and Entering
Zink - Attempted Assault on a city guard, damage of property. Reckless discharge of magic in the city.

Bada Bing is released from jail, and a T&C lawyer reaches out to him to inform him that he will help get his party off with minimal punishment and no prison, but it'll be tough and that what they really need is someone with enough beaurocratic sway and influence to vouch for the bugbear, or else it's very possible he'll end up in prison for assaulting the guard.

The party is thrown into a jail a series of jail cells and held with a number of other NPCs that have done crime. One of these NPCs may be a member of the Syndicate and recognize the party for blowing up the Factory 17 and the brawl with the city guard.

Over the next few days, the characters' trials will take place, where each will have to plead their case or be sentenced. 

#### What Happens

### In Jail
They meet [[Morgan "The Sluicer"]], who was thrown in jail and on his way to be escorted to the Iron Mines for attempting to preserve his memories of his employment with T&C with a recording device. Though, in the books they got him down for murder. He informs them of the bad doings on T&C and urges them to join the Syndicate to fight back against Big Corpo. Specifics he would know/why he personally hates T&C/what the syndicate is about:
> The Syndicate wants to wake the city up. T&C has you all in a dream of gold and gear, but the ground is screaming. I can’t remember my wife’s face, but I remember the pain... the screams of the very core of the planet itself as those drills dug deeper and deeper.

Party members are brought for questioning one at a time by Interrogator [[Gren]] (faithful, loyal, depressed). 
An earthquake hits while the party is in jail. A sinkhole is in the process of opening up, but hasn't yet. 

### Bing and Borgenarf's Release
Bada Bing is released along with Borgenarf III after seeing that no charges could really stick.
A T&C Lawyer, [[Silas Vane]] approaches Bing and informs him that they know of the kerfuffle, and the only way Boom is getting charges dropped for hitting a guard is by getting someone with sway to be able to vouch for the bugbear and they'll need to quick. 
	He paid off the judge, but supposedly, the plaintif was hired directly by Senator Grumblatch himself when he heard you all were hired by T&C. The Beaurocrats have been trying to get any dirt possible on T&C.

### Elysia Recovery
[[Arthur Olostina]] leaves his daughter alone to go on a business trip. He passes her a sending stone to use in case she needs anything, but will leave her with Merrick to watch over. He will be taking the estate guards with him, leaving the estate unguarded but Merrick will use illusions to make it seem protected.
`2-Story estate surrounding by a beautifully-kept couryard surrounding it with a culdesac-like driveway for carriages to pull through under a tower bridge. A large balcony sweeps across the 2nd floor providing an awning for the main entrance and stained glass windows adorn plentifully around the building.`

### The Trial
Each of the party members will be granted expedited trial since the prison wagon shall be arriving within the hour. Lurtz, Zink and Boom are all brought to the courtroom to trial. The plaintiff will bring evidence of their crime here, as well as past dirt on Bada Boom: specifically:
- His association with a ponzi scheme: `Call Bing and Ka-Ching` from a few years ago
- A property management scam involvement
They will bring a homeless man from Guttertown: `Rev. Frank Hooveter` to the stand who will accuse Boom of stealing his silver filling last week.

### Released
If the party succeeds at getting Boom off the hook, the judge will declare that Elysia must claim guardianship over the bugbear and he must attend behavioral therapy once a month. The party will be fined for damages.
T&C repercussions for them getting in trouble: Wages will be garnished at 25% each until each respective bail recovery is paid:
	- Lurtz: 150g
	- Boom: 600g
T&C will inform the party of rumors of strange fleshy creatures that emerged from the Factory 17 explosion that are devouring Guttertown locals and have even made it to the outer-gate Fenlight Slums. They will be given equipment to remedy what people have seen. They are responsible for interrogating the folks and making sure that anyone who saw anything... doesn't remember. And they will be given a device to wipe a continuous 10 minute memory of a creature of up to 3 days ago with 10 charges. Should they need more, they can put in a request with IT.
