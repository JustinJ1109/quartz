## **Overview**

Greyharbor is an inland city built along the churning banks of the Haddock River, a deep and powerful waterway that carved the land into [[The Gloamfen]] marshlands. Once a quiet riverine port, the city boomed into a roaring industrial center during the [[Arcano-Industrial Revolution]], fueled by magical innovation and endless resource extraction. The Haddock itself now acts as the lifeblood of the city — powering mills, carrying trade, and serving as a symbolic and physical divide between the city’s social classes.

Known across Enan as _The City of Industry_, Greyharbor is a place of smokestacks and clockwork, ambition and inequity. Its economy dominates the western continent, its streets are thick with soot and syndicates, and its towering skyline is broken by gears, cranes, and distant plumes of arcane exhaust.

---

## **Geographic Layout**

The Haddock River divides the city **horizontally**, with the **Upper Tier** rising on the western riverbank atop natural stone bluffs, and the **Lower Tier** sprawling across the eastern floodplain. Massive bridges and arcano-lifted tramlines span the river, connecting the two halves — but the cultural divide is harder to cross.

### Upper Tier
The Upper Tier sits elevated atop the river’s west bank, commanding a view of the entire valley basin. This district is the domain of the elite: corporate magnates, arcano-aristocrats, engineers, and clergy of [[The Machine God]]. Clean stone avenues, elevated rail lines, and glittering towers pierce the haze of the city below.

Key districts include:

- **The Gildspire** – Seat of city governance and home to the wealthiest citizens and seat of [[Castle Lothron]]. Polished brass towers, airship moorings and noble palaces are commonplace along the streets here.
    
- **Crankridge** – Arcano-engineering heart of the city; academies, inventor guilds, high-society workshops, and various shops and restaurants.
    
- **The Clockspire** – A towering mechanical monolith and timekeeping marvel; houses the [[Guild of Timewrights]] and state ministries, as well as many legal buildings for day-to-day beaurocratic activities.
    

---

### Lower Tier

On the Haddock’s eastern bank lies the city’s industrial engine — smokestacks, factories, slums, and steam-choked streets. Here, life is cheap and work is eternal.

Key districts include:

- **The Brassreach Yards** – Foundry blocks, smelters, and worker barracks; where machines and men alike are forged.
    
- **Guttertown** – A chaotic tangle of tenements, run-off gutters, and street gangs; crime is survival here.
    
- **The Lowdocks** – River loading stations, barge yards, and old fishing quarters; increasingly repurposed for cargo.
    

---

### **Outer Tier: The Metro Ring**

Beyond the city walls lies the outer sprawl — villages, shanty districts, and satellite communities that orbit Greyharbor's industrial gravity.

Prominent outer districts include:

- **East Slums** – Densely populated sprawl just beyond the Lower Tier gate. Overflow housing and outlaw enclaves.
    
- **Fenlight Yard** – Misty floodplain workshops and alchemical runoff zones. Strange lights and stranger disappearances.
    
- **Southwall** – Guarded gate town near the Upper Tier’s southern bridge. Heavily patrolled and militarized.
    
- **Shepherd’s Village** – A pastoral holdout near the Crankridge Gate. Sheep, mushrooms, and stubborn old magic.
    
- **Redgate** – A growing trade suburb near Crankridge, known for its mercantile families and shady contracts.
    

---

## **Cultural and Economic Notes**

Greyharbor thrives on invention — and suffers for it. Innovation and exploitation go hand-in-hand here, with the working class crushed beneath the gilded gears of progress. Labor unions war quietly with private security firms; experimental magic spills into tenement canals; nobles dine on spell-seared pheasant while goblin dockworkers drown in debts to their forge-masters.

Still, for many, Greyharbor represents _possibility_. A single invention, performance, or discovery could vault a nobody into power. The city rewards brilliance — and consumes it in equal measure.

---

## **Notable Organizations**

- **Thresher & Coil Industry** – (Now headquartered in Redvale) Originally rose to prominence in Greyharbor; still operates massive facilities here.
    
- **Guild of Timewrights** – Arcano-temporal guild headquartered in The Clockspire.
    
- **Iron Ring Union** – Semi-legal labor organization advocating for workers' rights.
    
- **The Vantine Trust** – Mysterious banking and mercantile syndicate based in Redgate.

## The Upper Tier

### 🎭 The Gildsprawl — Opulence & The Elite

- **Description**: Ornate aether-lit boulevards, flying carriages, noble spires, and glass-cast mansions.
    
- **Attractions**:
    
    - **High-Society Ball**: Attend a gala under false pretenses.
        
    - **Political Espionage**: Sway a noble house to support (or oppose) T&C.
        
    - **Putting on a Show**: Win favor by staging a performance in a lavish salon.
        

**Appeals to**: Roleplay-focused players, social maneuverers, intrigue lovers.

---
### 🧱 Crankridge — The Machinist Wards

- **Description**: Guild-run workshops, steel markets, gear-grinding alleys, and neighborhood pride.
    
- **Attractions**:
    
    - **Fighting Contest**: Combat tourneys hosted by local machinists.
        
    - **Turf War**: Help one guild win dominance over a rival.
        
    - **Crafting Challenge**: Forge something critical with rare parts.
        

**Appeals to**: Combat players, tinkerers, competitive characters.

**Reputation:**  
To the world, Greyharbor is the model of the modern age: advanced, influential, and relentless. But to those who live there, it’s a city of ghosts—haunted not by the dead, but by what was sacrificed to get here.

---
### 🕰️ The Clockspire — Government Core & Bureaucracy Labyrinth

- **Description**: Stone domes, brass timepieces, and layers upon layers of law offices.
    
- **Attractions**:
    
    - **Intrigue Web**: Navigate red tape to uncover corruption.
        
    - **Escort Mission**: Protect a whistleblower through government redistricts.
        
    - **Puzzle Challenge**: Decipher lost codes hidden in mechanical archives.
        

**Appeals to**: Intrigue lovers, puzzle-solvers, stealth/investigation types.


## The Lower Tier

### ⚙️ The Brassreach Yards — The Grand Factoryscape

- **Description**: Endless forges, conveyor lifts, and arcano-mechanical foundries. A place where the city breathes smoke and iron.
    
- **Attractions**:
    
    - **Infiltration Mission**: Break into the restricted zone of a T&C plant.
        
    - **Test of Mettle**: Prove your strength or earn work among brutal labor crews.
        
    - **Mystery Clue Hunt**: Discover what’s causing the strange aether sickness in the workers.
        

**Appeals to**: Combat lovers, intrigue players, exploration.

---

### 🛢️ Guttertown — The Workers' Underground

- **Description**: Stacked tenements, pipe-riddled alleys, union halls, and revolutionary graffiti.
    
- **Attractions**:
    
    - **Revolution Brewing**: Get roped into an uprising or help broker peace.
        
    - **Ball (of the Gutter)**: Attend a chaotic, underground gala of labor leaders.
        
    - **Gather Clues**: Uncover who’s selling out the resistance.
        

**Appeals to**: Roleplayers, intrigue fans, social climbers.

---

### 🌊 The Lowdocks — Smuggler’s Haven

- **Description**: Rusted harbors, hidden coves, canalways under smog, and black market trade.
    
- **Attractions**:
    
    - **Smuggler Heist**: Retrieve a crate the wrong people want.
        
    - **Infiltration/Exploration**: Traverse the flooded ruins beneath.
        
    - **Dispatch a Threat**: A sea-beast dwells beneath the docks, fed by waste…
        

**Appeals to**: Exploration, stealth, combat.

## 🎡 Extra Activities & Rotating Events

- **Aether Faire**: A seasonal street fair with strange food, oddities, and mini-games.
    
- **Clocktower Race**: Skiff race through the industrial skyline.
    
- **Riot Response**: Be caught in a protest gone wrong—pick a side or run.
    
- **Arcano-Accident**: Something explodes near you—investigate or escape?
    
- **Contract Board**: Side quests via mercenary contracts from factions.
    

---

## 🧭 Navigation and Player Loop

- **Travel**: Trams, cable-lifts, and cargo barques ferry players between districts.
    
- **Factions**: Each district is subtly controlled or contested by different factions (some known, some hidden).
    
- **Player Loop**: Encounter a lead or rumor → investigate via an attraction → discover a hidden layer of the city's mystery → choose a side or make enemies → unlock access to new zones.
