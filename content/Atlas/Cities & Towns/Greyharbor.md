## **Overview**

Greyharbor is an inland city built along the churning banks of the Haddock River, a deep and powerful waterway that carved the land into [[The Gloamfen]] marshlands. Once a quiet riverine port, the city boomed into a roaring industrial center during the [[Arcano-Industrial Revolution]], fueled by magical innovation and endless resource extraction. The Haddock itself now acts as the lifeblood of the city — powering mills, carrying trade, and serving as a symbolic and physical divide between the city’s social classes.

Known across Enan as _The City of Industry_, Greyharbor is a place of smokestacks and clockwork, ambition and inequity. Its economy dominates the western continent, its streets are thick with soot and syndicates, and its towering skyline is broken by gears, cranes, and distant plumes of arcane exhaust.

---

# Geographic Layout

The Haddock River divides the city **horizontally**, with the **Upper Tier** rising on the western riverbank atop natural stone bluffs, and the **Lower Tier** sprawling across the eastern floodplain. Massive bridges and arcano-lifted tramlines span the river, connecting the two halves — but the cultural divide is harder to cross.

## Upper Tier
The Upper Tier sits elevated atop the river’s west bank, commanding a view of the entire valley basin. This district is the domain of the elite: corporate magnates, arcano-aristocrats, engineers, and clergy of [[The Machine God]]. Clean stone avenues, elevated rail lines, and glittering towers pierce the haze of the city below.

Key districts include:

- **The Gildsprawl** – Seat of city governance and home to the wealthiest citizens and seat of [[Castle Lothron]]. Polished brass towers, airship moorings and noble palaces are commonplace along the streets here.
    
- **Crankridge** – Arcano-engineering heart of the city; academies, inventor guilds, high-society workshops, and various shops and restaurants.
    
- **The Clockspire** – A towering mechanical monolith and timekeeping marvel; houses the [[Guild of Timewrights]] and state ministries, as well as many legal buildings for day-to-day beaurocratic activities.

## Lower Tier

On the Haddock River’s eastern bank lies the city’s industrial engine — smokestacks, factories, slums, and steam-choked streets. Here, life is cheap and work is eternal.
### The Brassreach Yards
The **Brassreach Yards** are the churning, soot-stained lungs of Greyharbor. It is a labyrinth of fire-belching chimneys, high-voltage arcanomech coils, and miles of rusted catwalks.

Nearly every square inch of the Yards is owned by **Thresher & Coil Industries**, and for the inhabitants of Guttertown, Brassreach is both a life-preserver and a noose: it provides the only steady paycheck in the district, even if the work slowly kills you.

#### The Landscape of Industry
The Yards sit at the upper edge of the low district, south of the Haddock River, built out of slag and compacted waste. The air here is nearly thick enough to chew, tasting of sulfur, ozone, and burnt copper.

- **The Steel & Steam Sectors:** Massive foundries that run 24 hours a day, smelting the ore pulled from the deep-earth mines. The heat is so intense that the "Gutter-Shift" workers often lose their hair or eyebrows within the first month.
    
- **The Arcanomech Facilities:** These are the high-security blocks where T&C’s most secretive tech is assembled. Here, steam is fused with raw ether to power the city’s machinery. The sound of high-frequency humming is constant, often causing "The Itch"—a vibration in the bones that workers can't shake even after they go home.
    
- **The Airship Gallows:** Massive scaffolding structures where T&C’s fleet is serviced. Huge, soot-blackened dirigibles loom over the Yards like sleeping predators, blocking out the sun for the streets below.
#### The Environment: A Corporate Wasteland
Environmental safety is a luxury T&C has never bothered to buy.

- **The Haddock River Runoff:** The river surrounding the Yards is a swirling cocktail of iridescent oils and alchemical sludge. At night, the water glows a sickly, neon orange—locals joke that you don't need a torch to fish in the Haddock, but you might need a new pair of lungs if you eat what you catch.
    
- **The Iron Rain:** The constant smoke from the chimneys creates a localized weather system. A fine, black grit—"Iron Rain"—falls perpetually over the Yards and Guttertown, staining clothes, skin, and souls a permanent shade of charcoal.
    
- **The Toll of the Yards:** Long-term workers are easily identified by the "Brass-Cough," a wheezing, metallic rattle in their lungs caused by breathing the unfiltered fumes of the arcanomech vats.
#### Security and Control
The Yards are not a public space. They are a private corporate kingdom.

- **The Perimeter:** High iron fences topped with jagged, electrified wire surround the facility. Entrance is granted only through T&C-manned checkpoints where workers must present their "Life-Tabs" (employee ID tags).
    
- **The Shift Bells:** The sound of the Brassreach bells can be heard throughout Guttertown. They signal the start and end of the brutal 12-hour shifts. When the bells toll, the streets of Guttertown fill with a silent, soot-covered tide of men and women trudging toward the iron gates.
    
- **T&C Enforcers:** Unlike the City Guard, the T&C security teams are well-paid, well-fed, and heavily armed with steam-pressured batons and arcanomech shields. Their only job is to protect the machinery—the workers are considered an "expendable resource."
### **Guttertown**
Guttertown is the precarious foundation of Greyharbor. Situated at sea level along the black, oily banks of the **Haddock River**, it is a residential powerhouse of "slumstacks"—ramshackle apartment blocks that lean against one another for support, blocking out what little sun the smog doesn't already catch. This is the home of the city's disenfranchised working class, where the labor that fuels the C-Suites is lived out in cramped, drafty quarters.

The borough is a loud, crowded cesspool of survival. Because the **City Guard** considers the area a "low-priority sector," the streets are governed by the law of the coin. It is a neon-lit sprawl of unlicensed casinos, cheap liquor stores, and night markets where one can buy anything from a hot meal to a cold-blooded mercenary. If Greyharbor is the body of the world, Guttertown is its overworked heart.

##### The Low Docks

Hidden beneath the overhanging eaves of the slumstacks and the massive runoff pipes of the **Brassreach Yards** lies the **Low Docks**. This is the city’s true industrial drain—a two-block labyrinth of dripping masonry and rusted iron that sits partially submerged during high tide. While Guttertown belongs to the struggling workers, the Low Docks belongs to the **"Uncounted"**—those whose names have been scrubbed from the ledgers of society.

The district is defined by its permanent twilight. The air is thick with the smell of brine and alchemical sludge, illuminated only by the unstable flicker of dying ether-lamps and the eerie, phosphorescent glow of industrial runoff.

- **The Economy of Offal:** The Low Docks is the premier destination for the procurement of industrial secrets and stolen machinery. Every stall here is a fence, and every fence has a price.
    
- **The Collector:** This district is the permanent residence of the stout dwarven man known only as **The Collector**. A figure of both fear and necessity, he operates out of a workshop filled with jars of preserving fluid. He is the primary "doctor" for the locals, gracious enough to perform surgeries for free—provided he is allowed to keep whatever "questionable" body parts or metal fillings he extracts. He is always in the market for silver and gold teeth, no questions asked.
    
- **The Syndicate’s Reach:** The district's sole tavern—a hidden, windowless speakeasy—serves as a neutral ground for the city's most dangerous hitmen and smugglers. It is an open secret that the **Greywater Syndicate** runs the floor here; those who break the peace find themselves weighted with rusted iron and dropped into the Haddock River.
    
- **Authority:** The Greyharbor City Guard does not patrol the Low Docks. To enter these depths is to leave the protection of the law entirely.
