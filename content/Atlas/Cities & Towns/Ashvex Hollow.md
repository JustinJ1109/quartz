A chaotic sprawl of soot-blackened tenements, makeshift workshops, and rickety bridges over swampy channels of [[The Gloamfen]], **Ashvex Hollow** is a goblin-dominated industrial hub infamous for its volatile munitions foundries and lawless marketplaces. It was built atop the half-buried ruins of an ancient settlement, whose crumbling stones now serve as unstable foundations for the city’s ramshackle structures. Ashvex Hollow’s streets are alive with clattering contraptions, street hawkers, and mercenaries, with [[House Skeevin]] enforcing control through brutal pragmatism and back-alley deals.
