Built within a chain of massive limestone sinkholes at the edge of the flooded fenlands of [[The Gloamfen]], **Gravemire Bastion** serves as the industrial stronghold of the Goliath clans allied with [[The Ironwrought Assembly]]. Its open-air forges and titanic smelters burn day and night, producing heavy ironworks, siege weaponry, and armored constructs. The city is ringed by colossal bulwarks of stone and iron, and it hosts famed combat arenas where champions settle disputes in blood and steel.
