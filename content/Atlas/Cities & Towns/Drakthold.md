A labyrinthine hive-city of stacked workshops, steam vents, and winding subterranean passages, **Drakthold** is the foremost kobold enclave in [[The Gloamfen]]. Known for its relentless tinkering culture and cutthroat guild politics, the city specializes in small-scale arcano-mechanical devices, firearms, and explosives. Its streets twist and coil around central smokestacks and strange, haphazard towers cobbled together by generations of kobold engineers. Drakthold is both a marvel of innovation and a health hazard.
