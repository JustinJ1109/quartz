**Hearthvault** is the largest dwarven forge-city in [[The Gloamfen]] and a key industrial center of [[The Ironwrought Assembly]]. Built into the roots of an ancient petrified forest known as the **Emberwood Reaches**, Hearthvault is a sprawling subterranean complex of smelteries, arcane workshops, and stone-clad guild halls. It specializes in the production of heavy machinery, arcano-tech infrastructure, and enchanted alloys vital to both [[Greyharbor]]’s expansion and the Assembly’s war industries. The city’s streets are lit by ember-lanterns and lined with canals of molten slag, and its ruling council of master artisans answers directly to [[House Volmark]] and the Assembly’s Consul. Despite its industry, Hearthvault remains fiercely traditional, with deep ties to ancestor-worship and ironbound guild law.
