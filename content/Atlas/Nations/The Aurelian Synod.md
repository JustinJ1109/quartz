Radiant and ritual-bound, the **Aurelian Synod** is a celestial theocracy that governs by the rhythms of the heavens. Perched in the sky-piercing terraces of **[[The Spires of Valtarra]]**, its sanctuaries and observatories are places of solemn beauty and divine calculation. Here, prophecy is not mere poetry—it is law. The Synod is led by the **High Lumen**, a spiritual sovereign chosen through sacred augury and star-sign alignment, with Luminary **[[Serelith Virellan]]** currently holding the title.

Power is shared among the [[#Solar Orders]], each devoted to a celestial phase—**Dawn**, **Zenith**, **Dusk**, and **Eclipse**—and embodied by the noble houses that sponsor them. Their governance blends divine judgment with rigid tradition: calendars dictate legal proceedings, starlight readings determine military action, and sunfire rites bless every civic ceremony. To question the stars is to question the Synod itself.

Though outwardly serene, the Synod has stirred unease across Enan. Its [[#The Dawnbringers|Dawnbringers]] are both missionaries and militants, offering salvation with one hand and solar flame with the other. Whispers persist that the Synod’s relentless adherence to prophecy conceals a deeper aim—an astral convergence that may rewrite [[Enan]]’s fate under a reborn sun.

### Solar Orders

The Solar Orders are the core religious and military divisions within the Aurelian Synod, each representing one of the sun's four phases. These orders guide the theological, civic, and martial actions of the Synod. They uphold not only the teachings of the Synod but also the natural laws that govern Enan, as interpreted by the stars and celestial bodies. Every member of the Synod, from peasants to nobles, aligns with one of these Orders based on their birth, fate, or divine calling.

1. **Order of Dawn (The Awakened Light)**
    
    - **Focus**: Renewal, Growth, and Beginnings
        
    - **Symbol**: A rising sun with rays reaching outward
        
    - **Role**: The Order of Dawn represents the sun’s first light, bringing hope, life, and new beginnings. It is the most optimistic and nurturing of the Orders. They are responsible for the fertility rites, healing practices, and celebrations of renewal that mark the Synod’s calendar. They focus on spreading enlightenment, curing diseases, and promoting peace through knowledge. Their warriors fight with light-based magic that cleanses darkness.
        
    - **Notable Members**: Most farmers, healers, and oracles are trained under the Order of Dawn.
        
2. **Order of Zenith (The Burning Heart)**
    
    - **Focus**: Strength, Leadership, and Prosperity
        
    - **Symbol**: A blazing sun, a circle of fire
        
    - **Role**: The Order of Zenith is the most militant and authoritative Order, representing the peak of the sun’s power. Members of this order believe in the sun’s absolute right to rule and maintain order through strength. They lead military campaigns, oversee justice, and control the Synod’s most advanced military formations. Their weapons are imbued with radiant energy that can sear enemies or impose divine judgment.
        
    - **Notable Members**: The highest-ranking generals, judges, and political leaders are often members of the Order of Zenith.
        
3. **Order of Dusk (The Fading Flame)**
    
    - **Focus**: Reflection, Justice, and Fate
        
    - **Symbol**: A half-sunken sun with a fading glow
        
    - **Role**: The Order of Dusk is concerned with the passing of time, the judgments of the afterlife, and the preservation of balance. Members of this order serve as judges and arbiters, guiding the Synod’s legal framework. They maintain the ancient texts, and their role is also philosophical: they study the nature of mortality and the celestial balance between light and dark. Their warriors use defensive and reflective magic that mimics the waning light of the setting sun, often trapping enemies in illusions or snares.
        
    - **Notable Members**: Many high-ranking legal scholars, philosophers, and judges come from this Order.
        
4. **Order of Eclipse (The Hidden Sun)**
    
    - **Focus**: Secrecy, Transformation, and Mysticism
        
    - **Symbol**: A black disk overshadowing a sun
        
    - **Role**: The Order of Eclipse represents the hidden aspects of the sun, when it is temporarily veiled by shadow. It is often considered the most mysterious of the Orders, as it deals with the unseen aspects of the world—hidden knowledge, prophecy, and shadow magic. Members of the Eclipse Order often serve as spies, advisors, and interpreters of celestial omens. They are also the most feared because of their ability to manipulate darkness, illusion, and forbidden magic. Their warriors specialize in stealth, misdirection, and using the cover of darkness to strike decisively.
        
    - **Notable Members**: The high priests, visionaries, and those who interpret the most cryptic prophecies are part of this Order.

### The Dawnbringers

The **Dawnbringers** are a unique and elite group of missionaries, soldiers, and agents within the Aurelian Synod. Their mission is to spread the teachings of the Synod across Enan, not only through diplomacy and faith but also through military force when necessary. They are chosen from all four Solar Orders, depending on their alignment with the celestial phases, and are often sent on missions that involve significant upheaval—whether to convert hostile regions or to protect the Synod’s interests in distant lands.

- **Role**: The Dawnbringers are the face of the Synod’s expansionist efforts. They travel across Enan, converting new lands, spreading the belief in the Sun’s divine cycles, and offering protection to those who follow the celestial path. They are both revered and feared—seen as symbols of enlightenment but also as harbingers of destruction for those who resist the Synod’s doctrines.
    
- **Special Training**: Each Dawnbringer is rigorously trained in both celestial magic and martial combat. They wear mirrored armor that reflects the sun's radiance, and they wield weapons blessed by the Synod’s priests. They are often tasked with leading campaigns or leading missionary missions into unconverted regions.
    
- **Mission**: The ultimate mission of the Dawnbringers is to hasten the prophesied return of the [[Sun God]], as foretold by the Synod’s ancient texts. Their activities are not limited to peaceful proselytizing; they are often sent into regions in turmoil or conflict, and they have been known to justify military intervention through celestial mandates. Some say they bring salvation, while others claim they bring subjugation.
