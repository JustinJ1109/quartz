**Celestial Theocracy | Prophetic Dominion | Sunbound Hierarchs**

Radiant and austere, the Aurelian Synod is a theocratic empire devoted to the divine cycles of the sun, moon, and stars. Located in the golden highlands between the Halrun Wastes and the Dimming Expanse, this solar kingdom is ruled by the Hierophant Luminar—a living oracle said to commune directly with celestial powers. Power in the Synod is divided among Solar Orders, each representing a different aspect of the sun's phases: Dawn, Zenith, Dusk, and Eclipse.

Worship is not just religion here; it's law. Astrology, divine omens, and ritual are embedded into every level of civic life, from farming cycles to warfare. The Synod’s armies are clad in mirrored armor and wield radiant glyphs, trained from birth in both martial and theological doctrine. While insular and self-righteous, the Synod regularly sends missionaries and “dawnbringers” across Enan, offering protection, enlightenment—or forced conversion.

Many in Enan distrust the Synod’s intentions, particularly after several “sunscorch campaigns” justified by prophetic revelation. Some suspect the Synod’s obsession with celestial cycles masks a darker agenda tied to a prophesied return of a lost sun god.
