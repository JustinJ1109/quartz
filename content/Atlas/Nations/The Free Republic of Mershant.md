A coastal powerhouse of trade and invention, Mershant is a republic in name but governed by a powerful council of merchant princes, each representing major guilds and trade consortiums. The city-states that make up Mershant’s territory operate with significant autonomy, so long as they pay tribute and uphold the Republic’s mercantile laws. Wealth, not bloodline, determines one’s political clout—though many families have held sway for generations.

Mershant’s influence reaches far beyond its shores through its sprawling navy and mastery of magical shipping lanes. While officially neutral in many global conflicts, its fingers are in countless pies—supplying weapons, ships, and even magical contracts to both sides of a war if the profit margin is right. The Republic's attitude is summed up by a popular local saying: "There's no such thing as an honest coin, only a well-earned one."

Despite its glittering skylines and cosmopolitan markets, Mershant is rife with internal tension. Corruption festers beneath the surface, political rivalries often erupt into sabotage or bloodshed, and the gap between the haves and have-nots grows steadily. Still, it remains a symbol of freedom and ambition—where any adventurer, scoundrel, or genius might find fortune, infamy, or both.
