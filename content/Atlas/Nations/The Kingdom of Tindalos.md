The **Kingdom of Tindalos** is an esoteric monarchy located in the remote highlands and twilight forests of  **[[The Gloommere Highlands]]** in the souther region of [[Enan]]. Founded around the year 490, 3rd Age, its exact origins remain obscured by ancient oral traditions. The kingdom is home to a diverse population, primarily composed of halflings, gnomes, and those with fey-blooded ancestry. The capital, **[[Foxwick]]**, is a center of governance, where the monarch, known as the **Dreaming Crown**, resides.

The Kingdom operates under a unique system of government where the reigning monarch is chosen from among the royal family, but with a significant aspect of regency rotation. The monarch is not a permanent titleholder; rather, the position rotates among the royal houses in accordance with ancient traditions and prophecies. Currently, **Queen [[Primula Wyrmroot]]** holds the Dreaming Crown, having ascended to the throne in 1002, 4th Age. Under her rule, the kingdom continues to follow its fey-influenced practices, with seasonal rites and prophetic traditions guiding much of the kingdom’s policy and cultural practices.

The political landscape of the Kingdom of Tindalos is heavily influenced by its noble houses, including **[[House Wyrmroot]]**, the leading house with ties to the ancient fey magic of the land. **[[House Nywillow]]** is known for its connection to the prophetic traditions and rituals that guide the kingdom’s governance. **[[House Fernshade]]** remains a more enigmatic faction, often operating behind the scenes with a focus on the kingdom’s fey influences. These houses, while each distinct, work in concert to maintain the kingdom’s unique blend of magical and political traditions.

The Kingdom of Tindalos is marked by a strong connection to the fey realms, and its people maintain a deep respect for seasonal rites, celestial omens, and ancestral prophecies. While its practices may appear whimsical to outsiders, they are integral to the kingdom’s identity and governance, influencing everything from agriculture to diplomacy.
