The Dregahn are a proud people who dwell in stone-hewn fortress-holds nestled deep in the mountains. Governed by elders and spirits, each Holdfast operates semi-autonomously but answers to the High Moot in times of crisis. Their druidic traditions and rune-forged arms are deeply tied to their ancestors and the land. They often serve as mercenaries or smiths in foreign courts but by and large many tribes remain wary of outside influences.

Most Holdfast clans across Enan owe no allegiance to one another, nor to any authority beyond their own tribal kin. As a result, conflict is almost inevitable when tribes cross paths. Though research into their internal structures is scarce, some scholars estimate that as many as 80% of clans labeled under a given Holdfast operate independently, without formal ties to any larger tribal confederation.

While various clans have been reported around Enan, the vast majority of reported sightings and interactions with tribes that identify with the Holdfasts exist in the northern regions of Enan in the Nirunya Tundra.
