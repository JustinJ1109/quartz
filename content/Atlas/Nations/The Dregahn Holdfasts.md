The **Dregahn Holdfasts** are a federation of rugged, mountain-dwelling clans, deeply rooted in their ancestral lands, where the natural forces of earth, stone, and spirit intertwine with their culture. These clans operate semi-autonomously, each led by their own elders, shamans, and war-chiefs. Although they come together in times of great need to hold the High Moot—a gathering of clan leaders—their usual politics are driven by their kinship bonds and an unyielding respect for the land and its spirits. The stone itself is said to guide their decisions, a concept central to their worldview and belief systems.

At the heart of the Dregahn is their druidic tradition, which communes with the raw elements of the world, including the fierce mountain winds and the sacred fire of the earth. Many of their warriors wield runestone-forged weapons passed down from their forebears, carrying the deep magic of their ancestors. These ties to the land grant them a sense of pride and autonomy but also make them distrustful of outside influence. As a result, the Dregahn are often seen as mercenaries or artisans for foreign powers, but they remain cautious about engaging in politics beyond their own tribal structures. Their deeply spiritual connection with the land and their ability to work stone and metal has made them respected—though sometimes feared—across the continent.

Despite their lack of formal unity, the Dregahn Holdfasts occasionally unite under the banner of a leading clan during times of war or when their survival is at stake. Currently, **[[House Caerwyn]]** holds the leadership, bringing together various clans under a banner of more peaceful trade and negotiation. However, many other clans, such as **House Durnholde** and **House Thrainloch**, adhere to their independent ways, and often the Dregahn tribes clash over territories or resources, particularly in the mist-shrouded mountains of the **[[Nirunya Tundra]]**. These tribes are as varied in their ways as the land they inhabit, and while some seek to carve out a place in the wider world, others are content to remain hidden in the crags and valleys of the Dimming Expanse.

Though their internal structures remain elusive to outsiders, scholars speculate that as many as 80% of the tribes that are loosely associated with the Holdfasts remain independent, with no formal allegiance to a central authority. The true power of the Dregahn is found in their deep-rooted traditions, the mysterious stone they revere, and their fierce independence.
