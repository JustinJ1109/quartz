The Kezhari Bone-Courts are a loose alliance of nomadic tribes known for their skeletal motifs, shamanic spirit-binding traditions, and fierce mounted warfare. They operate under a code of honor and ancestral memory, with each tribe ruled by a Vohr who communes with their dead. While historically isolationist, increasing encroachment by industrial forces has led to political awakenings and the emergence of a unified spiritual resistance.
