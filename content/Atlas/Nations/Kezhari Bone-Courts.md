The Kezhari Bone-Courts are a strong alliance of individual nomadic tribes known for their skeletal motifs, shamanic spirit-binding traditions, and astral guidance rituals. Each of the Nine Courts functions independently, led by a _Bone-Chieftain_ who speaks not only for their people, but for the honored dead they ritually commune with. Their governance is oral, ancestral, and shamanic—wisdom passed down through rites, stories, and bone-carvings marked with the guidance of stars.

These tribes convene every two years beneath the shadow of the eternal monument known as **[[The Kezhar]]**, a colossal ribcage-shaped outcropping believed to be the bones of the first titan slain by the gods. There, they offer tribute, resolve disputes through ritual combat or divine trial, and honor their ancestors in great fireside rites. The current Bone-Chieftain—the highest spiritual and martial authority—is [[Saryk]], a battle-scarred mystic who rose to power through a rare unification of all Nine Courts under his banner.

While the Kezhari are insular and fiercely proud, their nomads are not unknown to the wider world. Bone-traders, sooth-carvers, and ritualists sometimes venture to border towns or trade in relics and knowledge—though always with caution. Outsiders rarely grasp the true complexity of Kezhari law, which flows more from dreams and signs than statutes or scripts.
