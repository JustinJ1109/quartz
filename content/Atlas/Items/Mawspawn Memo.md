**Thresher & Coil Industries**  
**Confidential Memorandum**  
**Subject:** Containment Review – Entity 17-B (“Mawspawn”)  
**Prepared for:** Consular Board Meeting

---

### Executive Summary

Entity 17-B (“Mawspawn”) breached containment protocols during the recent incident at Factory 17. Its physiology is unstable, semi-fluid, and marked by uncontrolled proliferative growth. Immediate civilian casualties were minimal during escape, but the threat profile is escalating as the entity migrates upward through the city.

T&C intelligence confirms fragments (“offspring”) have separated from the main mass and are manifesting erratically in lower-tier districts. These anomalies present increasing risk of discovery, panic, and political complication.

---

### Observed Traits

- **Composition:** Amorphous biomass with chitinous ridges and orifices resembling dentition. Fleshy, not crystalline.
    
- **Splitting Behavior:** Severe trauma causes entity to rupture, producing smaller but autonomous “mini-spawn.”
    
- **Regeneration:** Regrows mass by consuming organic matter. Larger host tissue yields accelerated growth.
    
- **Cognition:** Displays rudimentary pack intelligence. Mini-spawn appear to orient toward primary body.
    
- **Hazard Level:** If allowed to proliferate, entity poses existential threat to industrial quarters.
    

---

### Containment Directives

1. **Capture Preferred:** Central specimen must be retrieved alive for continued research in controlled facility.
    
2. **Secondary Priority:** Offshoots to be neutralized or recovered for analysis.
    
3. **Collateral Management:** Maintain plausible deniability. Reports to be framed as “rogue thaumaturgical discharge.”
    
4. **Engagement Protocol:** Standard combat methods are _discouraged_. Fire and acid exacerbate splitting. Electrical and freezing agents show promise in retarding replication.
    
5. **Asset Leverage:** Local contractors to be tasked with field operations. Their expendability limits corporate exposure.
    

---

### Strategic Considerations

- **Political:** Emergence near Clockspire or Gildsprawl would risk magistrate attention. Must conclude operation swiftly.
    
- **Public:** Witnesses are to be silenced or discredited. Memory suppression agents in short supply.
    
- **Operational:** Recommend deploying handlers to “observe” contractor team and ensure collection is executed as specified.
