**Designation:** 017-K
**Status:** Active Threat (Last sighting: East Slums furnace wards)  
**Classification:** Regenerative Pyroclastic Host  
**Summary:**  
Formerly human laborer subjected to thaumaturgic kiln-bonding experiments. Now a semi-coherent mass of fused flesh and smoldering stone, perpetually aflame from within.
**Behavioral Notes:**  
Attracted to sources of heat, fire, and organic material. Displays regenerative capabilities in presence of open flame or industrial heat sources. Vulnerable when cooled or submerged.
**Containment Priority:**  
Moderate. Recommended: cryo-gel dispensers or elemental dampening fields.
