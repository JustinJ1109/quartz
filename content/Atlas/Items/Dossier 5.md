**Designation:** 017-P
**Status:** Breach Confirmed (Location unknown)  
**Classification:** Parasitic Thought-Leech  
**Summary:**  
A writhing, centipede-like creature approximately 8 inches long, composed of faded parchment and ink-veined tissue. Burrows into a victim’s flesh and feeds on conscious thought, leaving victims alive but mentally inert and obedient.
**Behavioral Notes:**  
Prefers literate, educated targets. Victims found reciting nonsensical scripture or obsessively copying arcane diagrams.
**Containment Priority:**  
High. Immediate isolation of infected individuals. Removal of parasite is possible but risks permanent neurological degradation.
