### **Biomass Filtrator**

_Wondrous Item, rare, requires attunement_

This device resembles a heavy, rune-etched iron cylinder fitted with glass gauges, humming coils, and a bone-white aperture that pulses faintly when active. The Biomass Filtrator is designed to harvest and store unstable organic matter while filtering out inert or mundane tissue.

#### **Properties**

- **Activation.** As an action, you can activate the device, which emits a low thrum and projects a faint cone of translucent light out to 15 feet.

- While activated, it will automatically orient itself to the nearest instance of matching biomass within 2,000 feet.
    
- **Capture Biomass.** While active, any creature reduced to 0 hit points within direct line of sight of the cone must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have its unstable biological essence siphoned into the device. This does not prevent death saves, but prevents the body from leaving behind viable remains if the essence is captured.
    
- **Calibration.** As a bonus action, you can attempt to calibrate the device to a specific target creature you can see within 30 feet. Make an Intelligence (Arcana) check, DC equal to 10 + half the creature’s CR (rounded up). On a success, the Filtrator locks onto that biomass type. On a failure, it hums violently and cannot attempt to calibrate to the same creature for 1 hour.
	
- **Stability Warning.** If left full for more than 24 hours without being emptied, roll a d20 at dawn. On a 5 or lower, the biomass destabilizes: the device vents necrotic fumes in a 10-foot radius (6d6 necrotic damage, Con save DC 15 for half) and the mass begins to leak out over 3 turns until empty.
