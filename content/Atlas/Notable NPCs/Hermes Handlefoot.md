Hooded Goliath, member of [[The Greywater Syndicate]]. First met in [[Session Summaries/Session 6|Session 6]] when [[Bada Bing]] performed the Ballad of The Grinning Fool in the [[Mermaid's Keep]].

At the end of [[Session Summaries/Session 6|Session 6]], he was seen by [[Two Toed Pete]] escorting [[Goblino]] to a tavern in [[Greyharbor#The Low Docks|the Low Docks of Greyharbor]].
