The Collector is a stout dwarven man who resides in the [[Greyharbor#🛢️ Guttertown — The Workers' Underground|the Guttertown underbelly of Greyharbor]] in Trench Row Alley. He is a practicing dentist that used to reside in the [[Greyharbor#Crankridge|Crankridge]] district and ran a fairly esteemed dentistry establishment until he was removed from his position due to unlawful and unethical practices on patients. Now he runs an underground business of sorts, paying Bada Bing and Bada Boom for silver fillings they collect.
