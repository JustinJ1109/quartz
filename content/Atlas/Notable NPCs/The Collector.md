The Collector is a stout dwarven man who resides in the [[Greyharbor#🛢️ Guttertown — The Workers' Underground|the Guttertown underbelly of Greyharbor]] in Trench Row Alley. He is a practicing dentist and fence that pays Bada Bing and Bada Boom for silver fillings they collect.

===

He used to reside in the [[Greyharbor#Crankridge|Crankridge]] district and ran a fairly esteemed dentistry establishment until he was removed from his position due to unlawful and unethical practices on patients.

The Collector is a puppetmaster mafioso. He works closely with undertakers throughout the slums where he arranges donor bodies to come through him. He stores his wares by stitching them inside the corpses he gets and records their locations in a ledger.

He collects dirt on the party members:
- Has incriminating evidence proving that T&C was behind the Factory 17 bombing and directly ties the party to it as well as the cover up.
- Images, personal testimonies, and news reports from their Factory 17 endeavors
- Knows the whereabouts of family members and those closest to the party members
	- Lurtz' father
	- Grandma Fleck
	- Arthur Olostina
- Corrupt city guard enter the room and threaten the party

As the party talks, he might casually gesture to a pair of men moving crates in the corner:
“See those boys? Nothing fancy. Just doing what needs doing. You’d be surprised how well people behave when they know someone’s watchin’.”

If something violent happens nearby while he’s speaking:
“Excuse me a moment,” he says, then dispatches the threat with brutal efficiency before returning to conversation as if nothing happened.

“I’d hate to hurt anyone over a misunderstanding… but I'd hate it more if I failed to correct it.”
