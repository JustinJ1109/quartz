The Collector is a stout dwarven man who resides in the [[Greyharbor#🛢️ Guttertown — The Workers' Underground|the Guttertown underbelly of Greyharbor]] in Trench Row Alley. He is a practicing dentist and fence that pays Bada Bing and Bada Boom for silver fillings they collect.

===

He used to reside in the [[Greyharbor#Crankridge|Crankridge]] district and ran a fairly esteemed dentistry establishment until he was removed from his position due to unlawful and unethical practices on patients.
