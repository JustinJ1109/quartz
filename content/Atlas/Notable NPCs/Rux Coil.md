### Personality
- **Rough, Brash, & Uncompromising:** Rux is a **goblin brute** with a sharp tongue and a **pragmatic approach** to business. He’s all about getting his hands dirty, whether that means strong-arm tactics, dirty deals, or intimidating rivals. He's direct, blunt, and has zero patience for diplomacy. He’s the type to take what he wants, no finesse involved.
- **Street-Smart & Cunning:** Though he might lack the bookishness of Merrick, Rux has a cunning mind for **underground operations**, **black market dealings**, and **coercive tactics**. He’s the one who’s deeply involved in the shady side of the business—the back alleys, the unspoken threats, and the ways they deal with **disloyalty**.
- **Ruthless & Opportunistic:** Rux is always looking for the next quick profit or **easy advantage**. He’s not averse to stepping on people to climb the ladder, and his morality is flexible at best. He might be the one to pressure weaker factions into submission, using blackmail, bribery, or force to achieve their goals.
- **Loyal to Thresher, But Self-Serving:** While he is loyal to Merrick and the company, Rux’s loyalty is **more transactional**. He respects Merrick’s intellect and sees him as a useful partner, but Rux never forgets that the underworld runs on favors and paybacks. Rux does a lot of the "dirty" work because it keeps him in control, and he likes the power that comes with it.
