### Personality
- **Charming, Persuasive, & Eloquent:** Merrick is the smooth-talking face of Thresher & Coil. He’s the one who shakes hands with politicians, wooing investors with carefully chosen words. His voice is calm and measured, soothing but authoritative, a voice that commands respect without needing to raise it.
- **Manipulative & Calculating:** Beneath his charming exterior is a mind constantly working, calculating angles and spinning webs of deception. He’s not above using people or ideas to further his agenda—whether that means exploiting magical resources or manipulating public perception.
- **Intellectual & Artful:** Merrick’s love for magic is driven not just by power but by his intellectual pursuit. He’s deeply involved in the arcane arts—his research has led to technological innovations and, perhaps, terrifying magical breakthroughs. He’s a **man of letters**, always deeply involved in the theoretical aspects of their work.
- **Cold & Detached:** While he’s a social charmer, Merrick remains emotionally distant. He believes deeply in his company's vision of progress, but he sees people as tools. Those tools may be useful today, disposable tomorrow. He justifies his behavior by claiming his actions are for the greater good, but the “greater good” is often his idea of it, not the people’s.
