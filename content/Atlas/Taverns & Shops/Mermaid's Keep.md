A relatively upscale tavern on the border of Crankridge and Gildsprawl. 
There is a good-sized performance stage and a massive chandelier in the center of the room with mezzanine walkways surrounding looking down on the main floor leading to the various bedrooms.

Bada Bing performed the Grinning Song in [[Session Summaries/Session 6|Session 6]], and met the goliath [[Hermes Handlefoot]], a member of [[The Greywater Syndicate]] who drew them a map to [[Elysia Olostina]]'s estate.
