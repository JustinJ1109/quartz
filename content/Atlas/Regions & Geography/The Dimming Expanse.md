Bleak and starlit, the Dimming Expanse is a mysterious northern plateau where daylight rarely breaks through the heavy, shimmering clouds. The terrain ranges from tundra flats to frost-blasted cliffs, all permeated by an unnatural stillness. Strange phenomena plague the region—sky-borne lights, mirror-like ice fields, whispers in the wind—and locals believe the land is closer to other planes than the rest of Enan. Sparse settlements, reclusive orders, and ancient watchtowers dot the landscape. Expeditions into the Expanse vanish often, but the allure of arcane anomalies and untouched mythic sites ensures that someone is always willing to risk the dark.
