An impossibly vast mountain range of knife-edged peaks and crag-strewn valleys, the Spires of Valtarra are home to reclusive strongholds, hidden monasteries, and ancient stone-forged kingdoms. These heights were once sacred to the mountain clans and the spirits they revere—traditions still upheld in secret by many of the isolated peoples who live here. The terrain is treacherous and the weather merciless, but nestled within are massive stone cities carved directly into the cliffs, old mines rumored to still tap into magical ore veins, and ruins untouched since the Dawn Era. Airships and skybridges now link parts of the range, but much of Valtarra remains wild and unreachable.
