### **Overview**

The Gloamfen is a vast, misty region of swampy lowlands, where dense marshes, shadowed glades, and slow-moving rivers dominate the landscape. The wetland is situated on the northwestern side of [[Enan]]. At the heart of this treacherous land flows the Haddock River, which serves as both a lifeline and a dangerous obstacle for those navigating the Gloamfen. Its sluggish waters wind through the region, dividing settlements and providing a means of transport—albeit a perilous one, as the river is often home to Fen Serpents and other aquatic dangers. The constant fog and murk make the land seem eternally twilight, with strange lights from the Glowbulbs that dot the landscape only adding to the eerie ambiance.

Despite its wild beauty, the Gloamfen is a place of great peril. Its major settlement, [[Greyharbor]], serves as the industrial heart of the region, surrounded by factories and arcane rail hubs that contrast sharply with the untamed wilderness. Smaller settlements like Mirewatch and Latchbone cling to higher ground or float atop the marshes, struggling to survive in a land teeming with both magical and natural threats. The region is known for its strange blend of ancient druidic power, hag magic, and the encroaching forces of industry—creating a dangerous and unstable environment for any who venture too deep into its fog-covered reaches.

The Gloamfen is also home to the 4 major forge-cities that compose [[The Ironwrought Assembly]]. [[Hearthvault]], the dwarven city of the confederation, resides north of Greyharbor along the Haddock River. [[Drakthold]], the kobold forge city, is nestled deep in the eastern bog along the road towards [[The Gloommere Highlands]] built around and below [[Fen Lake]]. The goliath forge-city [[Gravemire Bastion]] sits atop the rolling hills above the wetlands to the north-eastern region nearest [[The Dimming Expanse]]. [[Ashvex Hollow]] took hold surrounding the ancient ruins to the southern edge of the region, preferring the warmer climate that the fen has to offer closer to [[The Mornreach Coast]]

### **Notable Dangers**

Travel in the Gloamfen is fraught with peril from both the environment and its inhabitants. The Haddock River, while vital for trade and transport, is home to numerous dangers. Fen Serpents lurk beneath the water's surface, while Trolls and Fomorians stalk the dense marshes, preying on the unwary. Additionally, the flora in the Gloamfen is just as dangerous, corpse flowers lurk along paths for unsuspecting prey.

The Gloamfen's foggy landscape can disorient even the most seasoned explorers, and its shifting wetlands have been known to swallow entire villages, causing widespread panic. In addition to these natural dangers, the region is rife with rumors of haunted ruins and the restless dead. Waterlogged Zombies, rising from ancient sunken graves, and eerie spirits that haunt the fog are common occurrences. The dense mist and shifting terrain have claimed many who have ventured too far into the marsh, making it a place few would willingly explore without good reason.

### **Notable Events**

1. **The Rise of Thresher & Coil in Greyharbor**  
    As the industrial heart of the Gloamfen, [[Greyharbor]] has seen the aggressive expansion of the powerful Thresher & Coil conglomerate. Under their influence, the city has rapidly transformed from a humble settlement into a sprawling industrial center, and their factories have begun to encroach on the surrounding wilderness. The rapid industrialization has already begun to damage the delicate balance of the Gloamfen's ecosystem. Some local druidic orders and even some of the ancient hag covens are beginning to mobilize against Thresher & Coil's encroachment, believing their unchecked expansion will bring about an environmental catastrophe. The battle between those who seek to protect the wilds and the relentless forces of industry is just beginning to unfold.
    
2. **The Vanishing of Mirewatch’s Founder**  
    The village of Mirewatch, located near the southern borders of the Gloamfen, has long been protected by a powerful druidic order led by its founder, Aelis Deepbough. However, several months ago, Aelis disappeared mysteriously during a full moon ritual meant to protect the village from marauding beastmen and spirits. The druids of Mirewatch have been searching for her ever since, but some fear that dark forces have taken her, or worse, that the land itself has claimed her as its own. In her absence, the once-peaceful settlement has faced rising tensions with the surrounding wilderness, and many fear the coming of something far worse than the usual swamp dangers.
    
3. **The Disappearance of Latchbone’s Leadership**  
    Latchbone, a small but industrious town on the Gloamfen’s western edge, is ruled by a loose council of merchant leaders and local power brokers. Recently, however, the entire council vanished overnight, with no sign of struggle or attack. The region has grown increasingly unstable as power vacuums form, and various factions from within the settlement and beyond now vie for control. Many suspect foul play, possibly involving one of the nearby hag covens or a rival faction of kobolds who seek to control the local supply of rare swamp herbs. The town remains in a state of uncertainty, with rumors swirling of a larger, dark conspiracy at work.
