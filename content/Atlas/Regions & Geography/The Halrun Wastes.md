>## The Halrun Wastes
>## - Overview -

>| ---- | ----- |

>| Flora | blightwheat, ashroot, veilthorn |

>| Common Inhabitants | Nomad clans, scavenger tribes, cursed remnants |

Once a fertile heartland, the Halrun Wastes are now a sun-blasted, blight-stricken expanse of fractured plains, jagged stone fields, and twisted ruins. The land bears the scars of ancient magical calamities—scarlet-glowing chasms, dust-storms that shimmer with arcane residue, and half-buried relics from long-dead empires. Sparse nomadic communities and scavenger clans survive here, often at odds with one another and with the strange predators that roam the ruins. Traveling through the Halrun is dangerous, but its secrets and forgotten treasures are enough to draw desperate adventurers, scholars, and profiteers alike.
