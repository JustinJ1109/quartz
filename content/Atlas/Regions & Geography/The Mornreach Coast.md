The Mornreach Coast stretches along the eastern edge of Enan, a region known for its glittering blue bays, trade-heavy port cities, and sprawling archipelagos. It’s one of the few places where politics, commerce, and culture mix freely between nations, though beneath its vibrant façade lie cutthroat merchant wars, naval espionage, and old feuds kept quiet by diplomacy. The coast is dominated by competing coastal republics, seafaring clans, and merchant consortiums, all of whom jealously guard their shipping routes and island holdings. Its cities are some of the wealthiest in Enan—gilded on the surface, but shadowed by ambition.
