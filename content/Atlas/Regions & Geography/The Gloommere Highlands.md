The **Gloommere Highlands** is an eastern region of rolling hills, mist-covered valleys, and densely wooded forests that lies at the edge of The Dimming Expanse, [[The Gloamfen]], and [[The Halrun Wastes]]. Though the land may appear dark and mysterious, its temperate climate offers a welcoming respite from the harsher landscapes of Enan. Known for its shifting fogs and the occasional ethereal glow of feylight, the Gloommere Highlands is home to numerous small villages and ancient, hidden groves.

The region is famed for its twilight landscapes, where the sun barely slips below the horizon in the spring and summer months, casting a soft, golden light over the land. Winters are gentle compared to the harsher regions of Enan, but the highlands remain veiled in a permanent aura of mystery. Travelers may encounter strange phenomena here—fey creatures wandering the woodlands, whispers carried on the wind, or unsettlingly serene lakes that seem to reflect not just the world around them, but a thousand possible futures.

Politically, the **Gloommere Highlands** is dominated by **[[The Kingdom of Tindalos]]**, whose capital, **[[Foxwick]]**, is nestled deep within the forests. The kingdom is ruled from the **Dreaming Crown**, a title passed between noble families based on ancient traditions and prophetic rites. However, the region's political landscape is also shaped by the fey energies that infuse the land, creating a subtle but ever-present tension between the mundane and the mystical. While the Kingdom of Tindalos maintains a firm grip on the highlands, other smaller fey courts, ancient woodland spirits, and semi-autonomous villages hold sway over their own territories.

Despite its magical aura, the Gloommere Highlands is not without its practicalities. The fertile land produces abundant crops of unusual herbs, berries, and enchanted flora used in both alchemy and potions. Timber from the enchanted forests is highly sought after, and skilled craftsmen from the Kingdom of Tindalos are known for their delicate, fey-infused woodwork. The region also serves as a trade route for more esoteric goods, such as rare magical artifacts and components tied to the ever-shifting rules of the feywild.

Though many fear the fey nature of the Gloommere Highlands, those who learn to respect its hidden rules and traditions often find it to be a place of great beauty and wonder. However, the constant whispers of prophecy, the lure of forgotten groves, and the eternal twilight may leave some travelers questioning whether they truly understand the land—or if the land is silently shaping their destiny.
