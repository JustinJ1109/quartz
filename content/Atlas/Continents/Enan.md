> _"A continent of fractured memory and ferocious ambition, where ancient roots grasp beneath new steel."_

Enan is a vast and volatile continent where the forces of nature, industry, and the arcane are in constant struggle. It is a place defined by contrasts: crumbling ruins and rising cities, sacred groves and smoke-belching factories, unity and discord. Each region bears its own distinct character and challenges, shaped by the terrain, its people, and the ambitions of those who seek to rule.

---

### **[[The Halrun Wastes]]** (South / Central)

Once a fertile heartland, the Halrun Wastes are now a cracked and sun-bleached expanse littered with bone-dry gullies, petrified forests, and ancient ruins half-buried in salt. This region is home to the **[[Kezhari Bone-Courts]]**, nomadic war-priests and bone-diviners who revere ancestral spirits and practice funerary magic. Their tribal confederacies clash as often with outsiders as with each other, and few dare trespass where the wind sings through skull-studded totems.

---

### **[[The Mornreach Coast]]** (South)

The shining jewel of Enan’s trade routes, the Mornreach Coast is known for its sunlit ports, windswept plateaus, and maritime cities that glitter with opportunity and intrigue. It is ruled in part by **[[The Free Republic of Mershant]]**, a mercantile oligarchy where merchant-princes and senate-appointed envoys govern with coin and charisma. The region thrives on trade but remains ever watchful for piracy, sabotage, or internal corruption. A crime syndicate known as **[[The Thousand Lanterns]]** maintains a strong presence among the coastal island towns and cities, posing a significant threat for maritime trade activities and have had rival territorial claims to the Free Republic of Mershant since the early 9th century of the 4th Age.

---

### **[[The Spires of Valtarra]]** (South East)

Jagged, soaring mountains cradle the Spires of Valtarra, where wind and stone conceal ancient monasteries, wyrm-haunted passes, and highland fortresses. This region is home to a handful of tribes hailing from the [[Kezhari Bone-Courts]] as well as [[The Aurelian Synod]], a theocratic nation of elves, aasimar, and humans nestled high upon massive plateaus and in crags upon the High Spire.

---

### **[[The Gloamfen]]** (North West)

A haunted mire of fog-laced swamps, black rivers, and half-sunken relics, the Gloamfen is as treacherous as it is beautiful. Home to the four forge-cities of each race (Kobold, Dwarf, Goliath, Goblin): [[Drakthold]], [[Hearthvault]], [[Gravemire Bastion]], and [[Ashvex Hollow]] as well as the capital city of **[[The Ironwrought Assembly]]**: [[Greyharbor]].

---

### **[[The Dimming Expanse]]** (North)

A windswept tundra where auroras dance above ice-shrouded forests and long-dead gods are said to lie buried, the Dimming Expanse remains Enan’s most mysterious region. Here, **[[The Dregahn Holdfasts]]** dwell in mountain-homes and redoubts, fighting not only the elements and beasts, but each other just as often.

---

### **[[The Gloommere Highlands]]** (East)

A temperate region of rolling hills, mist-covered valleys, and dense forests, located at the border of [[The Dimming Expanse]], [[The Gloamfen]], and [[The Halrun Wastes]]. Known for its ethereal feylight and perpetual twilight, the region is both mystical and fertile, home to [[The Kingdom of Tindalos]], whose capital is **[[Foxwick]]**. The land is shaped by fey energies, with ancient groves, hidden villages, and strange phenomena like magical creatures and prophetic whispers. While politically dominated by the Kingdom of Tindalos, various fey courts and semi-autonomous villages hold sway over their territories. The highlands produce rare herbs, enchanted flora, and prized timber, making it a hub for alchemical and magical goods. Despite its mystical aura, the Gloommere Highlands is known for its beauty, though travelers must respect its secretive rules and ancient traditions.
