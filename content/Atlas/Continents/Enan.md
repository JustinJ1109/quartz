> _"A continent of fractured memory and ferocious ambition, where ancient roots grasp beneath new steel."_

Enan is a vast and volatile continent where the forces of nature, industry, and the arcane are in constant struggle. It is a place defined by contrasts: crumbling ruins and rising cities, sacred groves and smoke-belching factories, unity and discord. Each region bears its own distinct character and challenges, shaped by the terrain, its people, and the ambitions of those who seek to rule.

---

### **[[The Halrun Wastes]]**

Once a fertile heartland, the Halrun Wastes are now a cracked and sun-bleached expanse littered with bone-dry gullies, petrified forests, and ancient ruins half-buried in salt. This region is home to the **[[Kezhari Bone-Courts]]**, nomadic war-priests and bone-diviners who revere ancestral spirits and practice funerary magic. Their tribal confederacies clash as often with outsiders as with each other, and few dare trespass where the wind sings through skull-studded totems.

---

### **[[The Mornreach Coast]]**

The shining jewel of Enan’s trade routes, the Mornreach Coast is known for its sunlit ports, windswept plateaus, and maritime cities that glitter with opportunity and intrigue. It is ruled in part by **[[The Free Republic of Mershant]]**, a mercantile oligarchy where merchant-princes and senate-appointed envoys govern with coin and charisma. The region thrives on trade but remains ever watchful for piracy, sabotage, or internal corruption. A crime syndicate known as [[The Thousand Lanterns]] maintains a strong presence among the coastal island towns and cities, posing a significant threat for maritime trade activities and have had rival territorial claims to the Free Republic of Mershant since the early 9th century of the 4th Age.

---

### **[[The Spires of Valtarra]]**

Jagged, soaring mountains cradle the Spires of Valtarra, where wind and stone conceal ancient monasteries, wyrm-haunted passes, and highland fortresses. This region is home to **[[The Dregahn Holdfasts]]**, a hardy network of mountain clans bound not by blood but by shared oaths to elemental spirits and ancient stone-forged laws. While the [[Kezhari Bone-Courts|Bone-Courts]] seek communion with the dead, the Dregahni revere the primal forces of the living world—storm, fire, and frost—marking a stark philosophical divide between the two peoples.

---

### **[[The Gloamfen]]**

A haunted mire of fog-laced swamps, black rivers, and half-sunken relics, the Gloamfen is as treacherous as it is beautiful. Settlements like [[Greyharbor]] thrive in uneasy harmony with their surroundings, defying the decay with industrial will. **[[Thresher & Coil Industry]]** has established a growing presence here, felling bogwood and draining marshes to fuel their arcane-technological ambitions—often at the expense of nature and the unseen things that dwell within. Druidic resistance festers beneath the canopy, quietly watching.

---

### **[[The Dimming Expanse]]**

A windswept tundra where auroras dance above ice-shrouded forests and long-dead gods are said to lie buried, the Dimming Expanse remains Enan’s most mysterious region. Here, **[[The Kingdom of Tindalos]]** rules from its hilltop barrows and standing stones, its halfling nobility cloaked in old rites and eldritch superstition. Nearby, **[[The Aurelian Synod]]**, a golden-theocratic order of sun-priests and philosophers, sends missionaries and warbands alike to bring “illumination” to the land—whether welcome or not.
