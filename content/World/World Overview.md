# Enan



## The Age of Verdance
> _“Before iron sang and smoke choked the skies.”_

- **Nature ruled the land**, and civilizations lived in harmony with elemental forces, fey courts, and primal spirits.
    
- The continent was broken into **tribal alliances**, **druidic circles**, and **arcane enclaves** that treated magic as a sacred, living force.
    
- **Leylines pulsed freely**, visible to the naked eye in some regions. Great beasts and elemental titans roamed freely.
    
- The land itself was considered alive—revered, feared, and rarely disturbed.

## The Age of Ascension
> _“Magic was mastered, and with mastery came ambition.”_

- Mortals began to **control arcane energy** through runes, rituals, and artificery.
    
- The **First Arcanum** formed—an empire built on magical infrastructure, teleportation gates, and arcane universities.
    
- Nature began to suffer. **Sacred groves were harvested**. Elementals were enslaved as laborers or power sources.
    
- **The Verdant Accord**, a coalition of druids and wild gods, rebelled, sparking the **Greenfire Wars** (~750 years ago).
    
#### 🟢 Greenfire Wars (~750–720 years ago)

- A brutal continent-wide conflict between industrial mages and nature factions. Forests burned; cities sank.
    
- Ended in a **stalemate** and the **Shattering of the Ley**, when a druidic ritual cracked the leyline network to halt industrial growth.
    
- Magic became **harder to access** in some areas, **chaotic** in others.

## The Age of Reclamation
> _“What was broken can be reforged.”_

- New powers emerged from the ashes: merchant guilds, city-states, and secretive technocratic societies.
    
- Efforts to **repair the leylines** began, but with **more control**, favoring urban centers.
    
- Nature retreated; its spirits became rarer, angrier, or went dormant.
    
- Arcane machinery saw a resurgence—**controlled**, not revered.
    
- **Thresher & Coil founded ~80 years ago** by Merrick’s grandfather as a small arcane tool workshop.

## The Age of Industry
> _“Progress through power. Vision through innovation.”_

- Thresher & Coil begins rapid expansion ~40 years ago under Merrick Thresher, fueled by **arcano-mechanical breakthroughs**.
    
- Magical creatures are now **resources**. Arcane refinement becomes a major industry.
    
- **The Company War (~20 years ago):** T&C outmaneuvers and absorbs several rival tech guilds.
    
- Local economies begin to depend entirely on Thresher & Coil, especially in the **south-central regions**.
    
- **Opposition movements** form—fragmented druids, disillusioned scholars, ex-workers, awakened spirits.

## The Spark of Rebellion
> _“Nature remembers. Magic resists.”_

- T&C has begun **invading unclaimed wildlands**, using legal and illegal means.
    
- Whispers of **spirit reawakening**, **leyline backlash**, and **ancient guardians stirring** circulate.
    
- **Rux Coil’s black ops** division is escalating covert projects: biological harvesting, mind-control tech, soul-energy refinement.
    
- Merrick Thresher prepares to unveil a **continent-wide arcane network** that would give them control over magic itself.
    
- The PCs’ story begins here—on the edge of empire, where the land still breathes… but not for long.
