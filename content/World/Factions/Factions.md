
## The Kezhari Bone-Courts
Inspiration: Mongolian and Central Asian steppes
Nomadic warbands / shamans

## The Varnathi Dustmarch
**Inspired by:** Ottoman and Persian empires
- A desert empire built on oasis cities and magical irrigation systems.
- Masters of glasswork, elemental bindings, and sand magic, with a ruling caliphate guided by dream-oracles.
- T&C wants their magical sand-forges and desert aetherium mines—but the Varnathi are not so easily bought.

### The Dregahn Holdfasts
**Inspired by:** Celtic and Norse tribes
- Mountain-dwelling clans who worship stone spirits and build cities in cliff faces and deep valleys.
- Fiercely independent, with a cultural reverence for craftsmanship and blood oaths.
- Some Dregahn have sold out to T&C for better tools and gold—others see them as abominations.

## The Free Republic of Mershant
- **Type:** Coastal Trade Power
- **Dominant Races:** Halflings, Humans, Tortles
- **Allegiance:** “Neutral” but economically entangled
- **Description:** Mershant’s politicians loudly claim neutrality, but their ports are full of T&C shipping crates and their coffers are overflowing. They're less ideologically aligned with T&C than they are _addicted to the economic boom_. They vote against any sanctions, and many T&C execs own summer estates here.

## The Kingdom of Vael'Dranar
- **Type:** Mystic Kingdom with Prophecy-Obsessed Nobles
- **Dominant Races:** Aasimar, Yuan-ti, Drow, Humans
- **Stance:** Split along philosophical lines
- **Description:** The Oracles of Vael’Dranar speak of an approaching “second sundering” where magic itself will be restructured. Some believe T&C's rise is part of this fate—others say it must be prevented. The nobility fund both resistance cells _and_ T&C-aligned research groups in secret. The kingdom itself is a dangerous web of mysticism and manipulation.

## Virdalith Enclave
- **Type:** Hidden Druidic Collective
- **Dominant Races:** Elves, Gnomes, Firbolgs, Tabaxi
- **Inspiration:** Classic fantasy druid groves + eco-terrorism
- **Description:** An ancient order deep in a primeval forest. They resist T&C through sabotage, summoning spirits, and spreading rumors of a “Green Reckoning.” Considered myth by many—but feared by the wise.

## The Thousand Lanterns
- **Type:** Merchant-Prince Syndicate
- **Dominant Races:** Aasimar, Tieflings, Humans
- **Inspiration:** Venice + underground black markets
- **Description:** Sprawling across coastal cities, this faction controls trade, secrets, and smuggling routes. T&C often works through them to move illegal goods—or sometimes _against_ them when interests collide.

## Kingdom of Tindalos
- **Type:** Feudal Monarchy
- **Dominant Race:** Halflings
- **Inspiration:** English countryside + eerie folk horror
- **Description:** Peaceful on the surface, the kingdom has old blood and older secrets. Some say the fields whisper, and the soil remembers. T&C’s intrusion has awakened something ancient that even the halflings dare not name.

## The Ironwrought Assembly 
- **Type:** Industrial Oligarchy  
- **Common Inhabitants:** Goblins, Kobolds, Gnomes, Dwarves  
- **Known For:** Smokestacks, slums, and ruthless industrial order
- **Description:** If you grew up in the Assembly, you probably didn’t grow up _well_. Life here means working in soot-choked factories, living six to a bunkroom, and learning quick that nobody cares unless you make them. The streets are loud, the sky’s always gray, and your only real options are to hustle, obey—or disappear.
	The Ironwrought Assembly is ruled by the rich and brilliant, a council of engineers and industrial barons who prize innovation over ethics. Thresher & Coil has deep ties here—some say this is where they first rose to power. You’ve probably seen their logo stamped on the machines that kept you fed, clothed… or nearly crushed.
	But if you’re smart, fast, or just mean enough, there’s always opportunity. Work a shady job. Pick a few pockets. Find a way up—or out. The Assembly doesn’t care how you survive, only that you don’t slow the gears.
