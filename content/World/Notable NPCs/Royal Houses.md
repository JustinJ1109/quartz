## 🪨 **The Dregahn Holdfasts**

_Mountain-forged, clan-bound nobility. Status is earned through lineage, craftsmanship, and deeds spoken in the Stonehall._

### 🛠 **House Brannoch-Stone**

- **Race:** Dwarves & Goliaths (intermixed)
    
- **Known For:** Forge-oaths, rune-carving, and highland diplomacy
    
- **Traits:** Stern, lawful, honorable to a fault
    
- **Notes:** Considered the most “noble” of the Dregahn—hold the ancestral Oathbell, used to call all clans to summit.
    

### 🕯 **House Caerwyn Hollowbind**

- **Race:** Humans and half-elves
    
- **Known For:** Mortuary rites, spirit-speaking, guarding the Veil between the living and dead
    
- **Traits:** Eerie, spiritual, revered
    
- **Notes:** Said to keep tombs that predate even the Dregahn clans. Some say they speak to stone ghosts.
    

### 🪓 **Clan Targhelm**

- **Race:** Bugbears & dwarves (old blood alliance)
    
- **Known For:** Monster-hunting, border defense, and tactical raiding
    
- **Traits:** Gruff, loyal, brutal in battle
    
- **Notes:** Though bugbears, they are respected for ancient service during the Oath War. Distrusted by some, honored by others.

## 🌵 **The Varnathi Dustmarch**

_Desert dynasties guided by elemental mastery, bloodline rituals, and ancient accords with bound spirits._

### 🦂 **House Sahradun**

- **Race:** Humans and tieflings
    
- **Known For:** Sand-binding magic, desert patrols, and war-mount breeding
    
- **Traits:** Proud, traditional, fiercely territorial
    
- **Notes:** Often provide military support to the Caliphate. Owns the Great Chasm Border Post.
    

### 🔮 **House Qazirid-Vel**

- **Race:** Aasimar
    
- **Known For:** Dream-divination and oasis sanctuaries
    
- **Traits:** Poetic, cryptic, high-ranking among the dream-oracles
    
- **Notes:** Members go into trance for months at a time. Some say their house is guided by a dreaming ancestor.
    

### 💎 **House Khurumbat**

- **Race:** Hobgoblins
    
- **Known For:** Jewel-mining, subterranean trade, and forging pacts with earth elementals
    
- **Traits:** Cunning, stern, well-dressed
    
- **Notes:** Though low-status historically, they now hold major influence due to aetherium wealth.

## 🏹 **The Kezhari Bone-Courts**

_Nomadic warbands, each clan governed by strength, spiritual favor, and ancestral glory. Nobility is spoken in titles won through trial._

### 🐺 **The Moonfang Kin**

- **Race:** Goblins and bugbears
    
- **Known For:** Beast-bonding, bonecraft, and war-mount breeding
    
- **Traits:** Intense, mystical, fiercely loyal
    
- **Notes:** Once exiled, now honored for saving a chieftain’s life. They ride direwolves and speak to the stars.
    

### 🔥 **The Ulaan-Yurak Banner**

- **Race:** Humans and orcs
    
- **Known For:** Fire shamans and the Great Ember Hunt
    
- **Traits:** Charismatic, loud, ceremonial
    
- **Notes:** Lead seasonal rites and the Skyfire Games. Known for bronze-plated armor and smoke-feather capes.
    

### 🌬 **The Silken Vultures**

- **Race:** Elves, tabaxi
    
- **Known For:** Spymastery, wind magic, and aerial scouts
    
- **Traits:** Elusive, stylish, sharp-tongued
    
- **Notes:** Travel by gliders and wind spirits. Some say they’ve never lost a war meeting.

## 🌾 **Kingdom of Tindalos**

_Feudal halfling realm steeped in folklore and eerie tranquility. Nobles rule over whispering hills and misty barrows._

### 🍂 **House Wyrmroot**

- **Race:** Halflings
    
- **Known For:** Barrow-watchers, ghost charmers, and hedge magic
    
- **Traits:** Polite, superstitious, respected
    
- **Notes:** The soil sings to them. Their family has protected cursed burial mounds for generations.
    

### 🕸 **House Nettlesilk**

- **Race:** Halflings and humans
    
- **Known For:** Taxation, contracts, and binding pacts
    
- **Traits:** Bureaucratic, impeccably dressed, quietly terrifying
    
- **Notes:** Whispered to make deals with things that dwell beneath the orchard roots.
    

### 🦇 **House Duskwake**

- **Race:** Half-elves
    
- **Known For:** Nocturnal patrols, owl-riding knights, moonlit courts
    
- **Traits:** Mysterious, noble, seen as cursed
    
- **Notes:** Seldom seen during the day. Have kept Tindalos safe from night-creeping horrors for centuries.

## ⚓ **Free Republic of Mershant**

_Rather than nobility, Mershant prizes wealthy “families of influence”—merchant dynasties who’ve controlled trade guilds and sea lanes for generations._

### 💰 **The Dorrival Syndic**

- **Race:** Halflings
    
- **Known For:** Controlling a third of Mershant’s shipping fleets
    
- **Traits:** Cheerful, ruthless negotiators, impeccably well-connected
    
- **Notes:** Known to host absurdly lavish feasts. Privately fund many coastal smugglers.
    

### 🐙 **The Elarven Trust**

- **Race:** Sea elves and humans
    
- **Known For:** Arcane shipping routes, tide-readers, and bonded kraken lore
    
- **Traits:** Eccentric, ceremonial, venerated
    
- **Notes:** Trace their lineage to an ocean pact long ago. Possibly immortal? Possibly lying?
    

### 🪶 **The Veksin Collective**

- **Race:** Goblins
    
- **Known For:** Running a network of couriers, thieves, and message-hawks
    
- **Traits:** Witty, fast-talking, information brokers
    
- **Notes:** Technically not noble, but feared and respected. Rumored to have dirt on everyone important.
