## Overview
Thresher & Coil (often just “T&C”) is the largest arcano-industrial company in the known world founded by business partners [[Merrick Thresher]] and [[Rux Coil]]. Born from humble workshop roots, it has grown into a sprawling megacorporation known for its innovations in magitech, infrastructure, and energy extraction. Its gleaming forges, clockwork engines, and aether-powered devices now fuel cities, war machines, and daily life alike across multiple continents.

#### Global Presence
T&C factories, depots, and rail lines stretch across much of the continent. Their branded crates and logos are as common as mud.

#### Cutting-Edge Tech
Everything from enchanted tools to arcane prosthetics, to security golems and skybound transports is produced by T&C factories all around Enan.

#### Corporate Neutrality
T&C claims to serve no king or country—just “the betterment of civilization.” (Of course, they have ties in plenty of royal courts.)

#### Workforce
They employ thousands—miners, mages, inventors, laborers, mercenaries. Wages vary. Safety regulations do too.

#### Mixed Reputation
Some owe their livelihoods to T&C. Others whisper about “disappearances,” “spirit-sick towns,” and strange experiments in remote facilities.

#### Logo & Motto
Logo: A bold, interlocking gear symbol flanked by ribbons of arcane energy with a happy goblin giving a thumbs up.

> "Progress Through Power, Vision Through Innovation"

## Faction Overview
**Thresher & Coil Industries** is a powerful, ambitious conglomerate at the forefront of industrial and magical innovation, led by the brilliant and calculating [[#Merrick Thresher]] and the ruthless goblin enforcer [[#Rux Coil]]. Specializing in the fusion of arcane and mechanical technologies, the company manufactures everything from enchanted machinery to magical resources, driving rapid expansion through aggressive acquisitions and environmental exploitation. With a reputation for cutting corners and using coercion to secure their interests, Thresher & Coil has become a force to be reckoned with, both revered and feared by those in the political and business spheres. Beneath their polished public image lies a web of corruption, illegal dealings, and a dark obsession with pushing the boundaries of magic and industry—at any cost.

### Company Presence / Prevalence
At the start of the campaign, **Thresher & Coil Industries** has a **dominant** presence in the **southern and central** regions of the continent, controlling large areas of land for their industrial sites. They have secured **major cities**, and their factories and technological outposts are scattered throughout these areas. Their influence is **growing quickly**, especially as they begin moving into **unclaimed territories** and **previously untouched lands**, which is the heart of the **conflict** in the campaign.

Their **expansion** is not just geographical but **influence-based**, moving into **political circles**, using bribery and charm to gain support from powerful figures while quashing resistance. The **rate of expansion** has been fast—both in terms of acquiring resources and alienating others, but their efforts are also **triggering tension** with local factions, resistance groups, and environmentalists, creating potential flashpoints. However, much of this is happening **behind the scenes** at the start of the campaign, with the party only gradually learning the scope of it.

### Company Goals
#### Short-Term Goals (1-3 Months)
- **Increase resource acquisition**: Expanding their control over valuable resources like **arcane minerals, rare magical plants, and magical beasts**.
- **Strengthen political influence**: Merrick’s role is to secure **new alliances**, bribe key political figures, and ensure they have a majority vote in influential councils.
- **Expand manufacturing capabilities**: They seek to build **new factories** and **innovation hubs** to churn out new magical-technological products.
**Blockers:**
- **Local resistance**: Environmentalists, nature-aligned factions, and smaller guilds are beginning to fight back against their industrial expansion. These groups are vocal and gaining support among the populace.
- **Resource scarcity**: Some areas are **not yielding as many resources** as they hoped, slowing down some of their expansion plans.
#### Mid-Term Goals (6 Months to 1 Year)
- **Consolidate territory**: They aim to secure **new lands** by neutralizing opposition through **diplomatic pressure**, force, or absorbing competing factions. This includes **dominating unclaimed wildlands** and **smaller factions**.
- **Magic-fueled industrial revolution**: Introduce **magical machinery** that **amplifies production**, creating new goods and reshaping industries in the region.
**Blockers:**
- **Resistance groups gaining traction**: Larger resistance groups are starting to form, banding together with **nature spirits** and **arcane scholars**. If they can’t get public support, the company could be seen as the villain, stalling progress.
- **Internal conflict**: As the company grows, **internal strife** between Thresher’s desire for **progress** and Coil’s more **brutal tactics** could surface, risking a power struggle.
#### Long-Term Goals (2-5 Years)
- **Dominance over the entire continent**: Thresher & Coil wants to establish **unquestioned dominance** over not just land, but the **entire economy**, transforming the region into an industrial powerhouse. This includes **complete control over magical resources** and **infrastructure**.
- **Become the sole provider of magical technology**: They want to corner the market on **arcane machinery** and **magical refinement**, positioning themselves as **indispensable** to both mundane and arcane society.
**Blockers:**
- **Growing rebellion**: As their territory and influence expand, so does the **opposition**. Environmentalists, old factions, and possibly **newly awakened magical creatures** or even **deities of nature** could rise up against them.
- **Political instability**: Their heavy-handed tactics could backfire, leading to **civil unrest** or even **civil war** if their competition grows too strong or if the public turns against them.
### Competition
The company faces competition, though it is often outclassed by the company's aggressive tactics and innovative edge. Their rivals include other magical-industrial conglomerates, smaller artisanal workshops, and nature-aligned factions who fiercely oppose the industrialization of the land. Rival factions often focus on more sustainable or traditional methods, while smaller competitors struggle to keep up with Thresher & Coil's ability to push boundaries with magic and machinery.

Ways of dealing with competition:
- Coercion and Sabotage
- Buyouts and Absorption
- Public Relations Manipulation

### Employees
**Employee Numbers:**  
Thresher & Coil employs **thousands** of workers across a wide variety of roles—from **magical engineers** and **artificers** to **manual laborers** and **guards** for their factories. Their workforce spans multiple regions, including those under direct control and those in newly acquired territories. This includes **skilled professionals** working on **arcane projects** and **grunt workers** in the factories. There’s a large **manual labor force**, especially those working in **dangerous environments** or **unsafe conditions** to harvest magical resources and minerals.

**Treatment of Employees:**
- **Skilled employees (engineers, artificers, researchers)** are often treated **well**, offered good pay and resources in exchange for their talents. However, their loyalty is still bought and maintained by **contracts**, and their creativity is often **restricted** to the company’s objectives.
- **Manual laborers and lower-class employees** are subjected to **harsh working conditions**. They work in **dangerous factories** or on dangerous mining expeditions, often **exploited for their labor** with little regard for safety. Working hours are long, and compensation is often poor, although there are promises of “advancement” if employees prove their worth.

**Employee Sentiment:**
- **Skilled employees**: Some are **loyal** to the vision of Thresher & Coil, motivated by **ambition** or the **chance to prove themselves** in a cutting-edge, high-tech company. But others feel **trapped**, forced into contracts with few options for leaving or advancing beyond their designated roles.
- **Lower-class employees**: They **resent** the company’s **exploitation**, especially after witnessing the effects of industrial expansion on **local environments** and **local communities**. Many stay because they have **few alternatives** or because the company promises **long-term benefits**, including future pay raises or promotion if they survive long enough.
**Why People Work for Them:**
- **Financial need**: For many, it’s a **job of necessity**. Thresher & Coil is one of the **few employers** in the region that offers **steady work**, even if it’s dangerous or unpleasant.
- **Ambition and opportunity**: Skilled employees, especially those in the arcane arts or mechanical fields, are attracted by the chance to work at the **forefront of magical industry**, contributing to **innovations** that may shape the future.
- **Fear of reprisal**: Especially in regions heavily controlled by Thresher & Coil, employees often feel that **refusing to work for them** could lead to **reprisals**—whether that’s being blacklisted, forced into dangerous labor, or becoming a target for Rux Coil’s more ruthless enforcers.

## [[Merrick Thresher]]
#### Role in the Business
- **Public Face & Arcane Innovation Lead:** Merrick is the one who **interacts with the public** and potential investors, presenting the company as a forward-thinking, clean, and highly competent entity. He’s also in charge of the **arcane research** and magical innovation that powers much of Thresher & Coil’s **industrial machinery**, like enchantments for their factories or spells to refine ores. He handles the **political alliances**, **deals**, and **academic partnerships**.
- **Image Control & Deception:** Merrick ensures that the company maintains its **shiny, reputable image** to the public. He’s the one smoothing over the less palatable aspects of their operations, like the environmental destruction, by framing them as **necessary progress**. He might even fund charitable causes as a way of improving the company's **public relations**.
## [[Rux Coil]]
#### Role in the Business
- **Enforcer & Underworld Operations Lead:** Rux is the one handling **illegal operations**, **coercion**, and **black market dealings**. If someone’s standing in the way of progress or challenging Thresher & Coil’s expansion, Rux is the one sent to "convince" them to step aside. He’s deeply involved in making sure the **resources** and **raw materials** come through—at any cost.
- **Securing Power & Alliances:** He doesn’t shy away from **extortion** or **smuggling** to fund their operations. Whether it’s pushing around the competition or convincing local gangs or factions to sell out, Rux is the one ensuring their **economic foothold** is solid in both the legal and illegal markets. He handles **security** for the company’s more dangerous projects and **wielding fear** to maintain order in the ranks.
- **Conflict Management:** Whenever something goes wrong—either an unsanctioned rebellion or a competitor trying to muscle in—Rux is the first one to step in and solve the problem with a mixture of violence and dirty tricks.
## Potential Conflict or Tension
Despite their partnership, there could be tension between the two:
- **Merrick** may feel that **Rux** is getting too involved in the "messy" parts of the business, especially when it affects their **public image**.
    
- **Rux** might view **Merrick** as **too idealistic** or out of touch with the real world—preferring to keep things _simple_ and _profitable_ rather than worrying about how they look to the public.
