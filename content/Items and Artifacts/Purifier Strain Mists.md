Consumable, rare

Sold as a “cleansing vapor” for areas affected by magical corruption or plague. Actually releases an arcane mist that eradicates biological material indiscriminately. Used in regions marked for industrial takeover to clear “hazard zones.”

Locals call it “The Gray Rain.” It smells faintly like lavender and ozone.

Towns that are near areas where Gray Rain is used are given days notice on when to remain indoors for the duration of the construction.

The use of the mist is illegal in most areas outside of [[Thresher & Coil Industry]] influence.
